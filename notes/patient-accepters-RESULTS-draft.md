*Mechanism (inferred).* The RS's program is right about the edges: R is a neutral shadow of the ⅓ constant on a quiet unequal island, and each route's second step is strongly selected.
- Three things stop it at these rates. First, R's patience can be answered only by a non-reading ½ constant: every reader's offer times out, so O and F cannot be offerers.
- Second, R's own reading makes it illegible to the bully whenever any reader is nearby. The victim seat with R is then lost (the bully drops it), and R is selected out of the scramble rather than resting in the victim's seat.
- Third, both routes need two rare single-slot steps in sequence, the first paced by a tail arrival times 1/N and the second by an arrival times 10–28/N. As a heuristic only, the product of the two per-arrival rates is 3·10⁻⁷ to 10⁻⁶ against a front-loaded tail; the runs do not isolate migration's dilution of a single island's change.
- R does add something: it is part of the fair consensus where islands go fair, it is free without bullies, and more of it helps a little. But the unequal basin is still set in the founding scramble, as in queue 76.

*What it says about the long-term goal.* A patient accepter is as cheap as a bully in the length prior and enters unequal islands neutrally. Its offerers must be programs it can read, which in arm T means non-readers. Reading victims also become unreadable to their partners. For realizable source-reading programs the obstacle is the regress of mutual reading, not prior mass. A patient accepter that triggers on reader offers needs a reading semantics that terminates between readers (a certified or fixed-point offer), and keeping it a constant to its current partner needs a way to answer one partner without reading the other first. Both are outside arm T's capped simulation.
