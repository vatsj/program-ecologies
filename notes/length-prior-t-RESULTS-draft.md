*Step 1: the prior.* A branching-probability prior over bodies of the arm-T frame: tags uniform among the machine's tags, de Bruijn indices and literals Elias-gamma. Machine R0 = the 18 tags of `lt_code` (a read is written out as `(capk k (run k n p) me)`); R1 = R0 plus two arity-0 read tags. Both are supercritical (mean arity 4/3 and 6/5; finite-tree mass q = 0.4748 and 2/3; the rest is infinite trees). Classification is behavioural, by output and charge signature on a 29 x 29 probe table (the 27 library programs and the 2 Trojan bullies), exact table match to the 31 members of T+ (27 programs, two bullies, two junk programs) and a response-map family label; a partner-independence tracker and R1's read-outcome shortcuts make the enumeration feasible. Exhaustive to s0 = 6 (R0, 2.1e8 terms) and 5 (R1, 7.7e6) with the mass accounted to 1 within 1e-13 (enumerated + terms with lib / libsrc / sim tags + above s0 + infinite trees); Monte Carlo from the prior (12e6 and 6e6 draws) for what is not enumerated; a decision-tree grammar for the reader classes, giving lower bounds. *What it says about the long-term goal.* Realizable source-reading cooperation needs readers, and a length prior over this Turing-complete syntax gives them at most 1.3·10⁻⁵ of the constants' mass (95% bound; grammar lower bounds 4·10⁻¹⁰ with a read primitive, 3·10⁻²³ with reading written out), in a prior that is 72–88% programs that forfeit. The bounds overlap, so no reading-cost effect of a given size is established. The smallest tested successful reader share is 10⁻⁴ (at β = 0; no positive lower threshold was measured at (100, 256)), and at the measured bully ratio no tested share succeeds. The result does not say the Solomonoff prior fails: it says that the *hand-set* library prior of arm T was carrying the finding (a 9% reader share and no bullies), and that a length prior over this syntax puts the weight on junk, on constants whose mass is set by the Elias code, and on conditionals that are not the library's.

*Caveats.* s0 = 6 / 5 against the spec's 9; the reader lower bounds are of a grammar, not complete; lib / libsrc / sim terms are sampled, not enumerated; the machine is two points on one axis (the cost of reading); the join is a projection onto T+ (other conditionals, 15% of R1's finite mass, are omitted); finite schedules.
