- **Bully resistance in arm T (queue 76):** **P / P_z / P_o (`P<dir><fb><to>`)** — the prudent reader: accept the read partner j at 1/2 iff the cooperation read says j names me at 1/2 *and* a second read of j with the third slot replaced by the constant that names j at 1/3 (C3 for dir n, C0 for dir p) does not show j naming the third slot at 2/3 (code 2 for n, 5 for p); strict probe (an inconclusive probe read refuses), fallback z (null) or o (the other slot at 1/2); **Q (`Q<dir>z<to>`)** — the lenient-probe variant (an inconclusive probe read accepts); **O (`O<first><fb><to>`)** — the opener: read both others; if j names k at 2/3 and k names j at 1/3 name k at 1/2 (the 1/3-holder), else act as the two-partner reader D; **F2 (`F<dir>z<to>`)** — the matched firm control (T's single reader plus P's second read made and ignored, so it pays P's read charges without discriminating); **firm reader / F** — T's own single and two-partner readers (they never offer 1/3); **K = 55** — the catalogue T+ ∪ P ∪ Q ∪ O ∪ F2 (the first 31 programs are T+'s, unchanged).
- **Step-0 assays:** **rho * N** — N times the exact fixation probability of one invader copy in a single island whose other two slots are monomorphic, rho = (1 − 1/r)/(1 − 1/r^N), r = exp(w d), d = the invader's net (A_f-charged) payoff minus the resident's in the invaded slot; 1 is neutral; **FI / FC / UB / BF** — assay classes: bully into fair islands held by firm readers in all three slots / by two readers forming the pair plus a constant; a firm reader (or P, O) into a bully-held unequal island (a bully on 2/3 with a 1/3-naming constant); the 1/3 constants C0, C3, C6 into a bully-held fair island; **pair slot / unpaired slot** — whether the invaded slot has a positive share in the resident outcome; **holding configuration** — the dominant program per slot of an island's modal triple.
- **R2 (queue 76):** R1 plus two probe tags **`probn(c)`, `probp(c)`** (arity 1, literal child c in 0..8): the written-out read of the partner with the third slot replaced by the quoted constant program of code c (same caps); a non-literal child is stuck; **table collision** — two members with identical probe tables (Qnzn = Pnzn, Qpzn = Ppzn, Fnzn = Snzn, Fpzn = Spzn), classified as the first; **vacuous-probe spelling** — a spelling of a T+ class that contains a probe read whose result does not change the output (counted separately from the T+ search).
- **Idealized uncapped table:** level iteration from the all-TO bottom with reads answered by the partner's output one level earlier (a probe by the output against the stand-in constant); cells that still change after 30 levels are frozen at the timeout action 8; no charges. A diagnostic that separates strategic from timeout-induced behaviour (suppressed-charge replay: the same capped actions with the charges set to zero).
- **Length-prior-shaped series (2c):** the series 2b law over T+ ∪ P ∪ O: constants, junk, F, P, O, bullies at their measured exact-member lower-bound masses (conditional on finite trees), the reader share delta = F + P + O in the measured within-reader ratio, bullies at (bully mass) / (reader mass) times delta; the unclassified remainder allocated **to constants** (junk fixed at its measure) or **to junk** (constants fixed at theirs).
