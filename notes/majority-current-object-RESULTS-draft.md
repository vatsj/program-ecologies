*Question and object.* The OH doc's Theory tab (§3 a = 1) asked for majority division in the object as the RS defined it on 2026-11-01: the seed lottery with the annealed tail, finite N and I, migration, arms M and T. The recorded predictions were the RS's ("does not land in V\*") and the RE's ("agreed for the DSL arms under the length prior; for arm T, V\* on at least 0.9 of islands at N ≤ 200, unresolved at N = 400"). Object: `dollar3` (⅓ – ½ – ⅔ grid), three slot populations of N per island, I = 64 islands, Moran births with fitness exp(0.3·payoff), migration m = mN/N per birth from a uniformly random other island. The tail: from g₀ = 2N, Poisson arrivals with mean Λ[(1 + (g − g₀)/t₀)⁻¹ − (1 + (g + 1 − g₀)/t₀)⁻¹] per island and generation (t₀ = N/2) into a uniformly random slot, each drawn from that slot's founding seed law; 100N generations plus a 20N tail-free chase. *What it means.* (finite-schedule) (a) In the current object at these sizes arm T meets the stable-set target at mN = 1 in every run and cell, with each fair pair about equally likely across worlds at N ≤ 200: the mean-field result of queue 69 and the RE's recorded prediction carry over to N = 100–400 at I = 64 with the annealed tail up to Λ = 100. The RS's recorded prediction fails for T and holds for the DSL arms. (b) The DSL arms do not land under the length prior (the free oracle and the weak evaluator are not distinguished), and their failure is the founding supply of ⅓-naming constants and Trojan bullies, which the prior's own tail does not repair (queue 71's mean-field mechanism, here confirmed at finite N and I as a founding effect). (c) T's success depends on its readers: the constants alone fall with N like the DSL arms. So what is compared is two packages (library prior with 10% readers and no bullies, A_f charge, capped simulation, against the length prior with the free sound box), and the next question the run points at is h03's exception: whether the library prior's reader mass and bully-free supply survive in a length prior over the real syntax.

*Caveats.* One prior per arm, one grid (⅓ – ½ – ⅔), I = 64 in one component, finite schedules (the 240N long chase moves nothing, but a window shorter than the mixing time is not π); N = 400 has 20 runs, so M's cells there have wide intervals and the "patchwork" reading rests on 10 of 20 mixed runs; establishment is by presence episode, not lineage; T0's constants are uniform over nine, a different constant law from the length prior's (also uniform over nine, but with 10⁻⁵ of bullies); `modal` and the majority-triple series have identical cell means and ties occur on ≤ 0.16% of island-snapshots.

*Model cannot express.* Lineage-tagged establishment (the kernel counts presence episodes); a length prior over the realizable syntax for arm T (h03's exception stands); a charged cost for arm M (the free oracle has no failed reads, so the M–T contrast confounds reading's cost with the prior and the evaluator).
