## RESULTS draft

### The pair-worth-a game on islands: easy case, middle case, glove market, hard case with grand bids

*Question and design (for a reader days later).* The RS's conjecture for the easy case (pair worth a = 1/3, core externally stable in the continuum): under the seed lottery with the annealed tail every island ends in the core and every core point appears with positive probability. The spec adds the middle case (a = 2/3, glove monopolist), the hard case (a = 5/6, empty core) and *grand bids* (a bid names the grand coalition at a full split on the condition that both others provably do, with an exclusion-pair fallback), with blockers K, accept-pair bids A, grand constants G and readers R as controls. Islands: three slot populations, N = 100, I = 64, w = 0.3, mean-field payoffs, migration mN in {1, 0, 0.1}, 12,000 generations, annealed tail Lambda = 10 from generation 200 (and Lambda = 0), 20 runs per cell with founders paired across arms, tails and mN (runs 20-59 added for the key cells). Programs are decision lists over provability atoms on the GL chain (V5: checked against an independent evaluator and against `dollar3.eval_modal`).

*A design finding before any run.* As written the bid's exclusion fallback never fires: its grand line is vacuously true at world 0, so every bid plays the grand action at world 0 and the pair-handshake atoms (level 0) can never hold afterwards (hand derivation, prototype, V4). The run therefore reads the handshake atoms at level 1 (PA-provability of a Con-conditional); the as-written bids are controls (B0). With the repair, the spec's index-ordered partner priority makes K slot-asymmetric (V6: the permutation check fails at 17% of random triples as stated, and passes with priorities carried).

*Static numbers.* Strict undominated outcomes on the grid: G1/3 55 = 19 core + 6 ring + 30 undominated wasteful (weak: the 19 core points, unique stable set); G2/3 13 = 1 + 9 + 3; glove 5 = 1 + 3 + 1; G5/6 0, with a unique strict stable set of 15 vectors and none under weak. The Theory tab's grid claim (strict undominated = core proper at 24ths) is false: on 24ths the counts are the same, 13 and 5; the ring is one tick wide at every grid.

*Results.*
1. **The RS's conjecture fails in the form scored.** G1/3, C+B* (bids on all 28 splits, dose 0.05), Lambda 10, mN 1: core proper 0.25 [0.05, 0.45] of islands (20 runs; 0.25 [0.15, 0.37] over 60), against the 0.95 threshold. Every run is a global consensus (0.95 of runs) on one outcome: either the equal-split grand coalition (core) or a wasteful pair {1,1,0}. At mN = 0, 0.78 of islands end in the null outcome (nobody coordinates), core proper 0.02, and 11 of 19 core points are ever observed; all 19 have simultaneous upper bounds below 1/76. Zero-share core points are under-represented 3x relative to positive-share ones (as argon predicted for coverage; his 0.9 core share at mN 1 fails: C+B 0.15).
2. **Bids do not help at N = 100 and help at N >= 200.** G1/3 mN 1, Lambda 10: C 0.45 [0.25, 0.65] (0.53 over 60 runs), C+B* 0.25, C+B 0.15 (0.17 over 60), and **C+G (28 grand constants, no reading) 0.80 [0.60, 0.95]**; so the paired difference C+B* - C is -0.20 [-0.45, 0.05] (−0.28 over 60) and C+B* - C+G is -0.55 [-0.75, -0.35] (RE2 predicted +0.15 or more). At N = 200 C+B* is 0.80 [0.60, 0.95] against C 0.56 [0.35, 0.76]; at N = 400 0.85 [0.70, 1.00] against 0.33 [0.21, 0.46]: the pattern the spec predicted appears once the per-split seed count per slot is above one. Bids on a single split (the equal split, dose 0.05, C+B=) put the core at **1.00 of islands in 20 of 20 runs** at mN 1 (but only the one core point is ever observed, and only 0.03 core at mN = 0): the conjecture's menu confound is large, exactly the one the spec flagged. Raising the dose with 28 splits does not help (0.30: 0.18; 0.50: 0.05 core, pair 0.95): bids on different splits of different slots pair at the exclusion split instead of coordinating. Level-0 bids (no working fallback) are not distinguishable from C at 20 runs (0.15 vs 0.45 at mN 1, interval includes 0). Accept-pair bids hurt (G1/3 mN 1 core 0.00, pair 0.95-1.00).
3. **The tail does nothing.** Lambda 1, 10 and 100 give the same end shares within the run-to-run interval in every cell; the grand-state escape hazard is 0 in 2.0·10^6 island-generations (G1/3) and 2-4·10^-6 per generation at G5/6 (4 and 8 exits in 200 islands), so grand states are long-lived, not absorbing, as sol predicted, but longer-lived than any run.
4. **The ring never appears and transfers change nothing.** Ring islands 0.00-0.01 in every cell; on the 24ths grid (G2/3', glove') the end states are the same as on sixths. The one-tick ring is statically undominated but is not where islands end; islands end on dominated outcomes.
5. **The middle case ends on dominated outcomes.** G2/3: 1.00 of islands at mN 1 end on dominated wasteful pairs, core 0.00, in C and C+B. Glove: the monopolist's mean share is 0.62 (C) and 0.75 (C+B); islands end on dominated efficient splits (0.95) or the ring (0.55 in C+B); (1,0,0) never, the core is unreachable, and the Bertrand ring (5/6,1/6,0) appears in C+B only (0.55 [0.35, 0.75]).
6. **The hard case is a pair state under any migration.** G5/6 C, C+B, C+B+K, C+B2, C+G, C+A, R, R+B: 1.00 of islands end in a pair at mN 1 and 0.1, with and without the tail; grand-split islands appear only at mN = 0 and only at a high dose (0.37 at dose 0.3, 0.72 at dose 0.5 for the equal-split bid). The assays say why: on a B^3 island at a = 5/6 a pair constant that offers the exclusion split (5,5) fixes at 2.7/N, since the bids accept it, so any migrant or arrival that offers the pair beats the grand split (5/12 against 4/12); one blocker fixes at 2.7/N in slot 0 or 1 and never in slot 2. No grand <-> pair cycling is observed because no grand state survives; switches per island per 10^4 generations are 0.2-0.9 (null <-> pair), 0.0 in the chase.
7. **Invasion assays (N = 100, 2,000 replicates and the exact Moran value).** A bid in a pair slot is neutral (N·p = 1.1, 1.1 for equal pairs; 0.65-1.15 on unequal pairs). With bids in both pair slots a bid in the excluded slot fixes at 10.65/N (exact 9.52), but **so does the matching grand constant** (9.2/N): once two slots bid, the third needs a coordination device, not a reader. A matching grand constant on a B^3 island is neutral (1.0-1.2); an accept-pair bid and a level-0 bid are neutral; **a blocker is not**: at G5/6 it fixes at 2.7/N in slots 0 and 1 and 0/N in slot 2 (exact 2.69, 2.69, 0.00); at G1/3 it never fixes (it loses its 1/6). K^2 with a third slot: the third bid is lost only when the third slot is slot 2 (0.85/N); with the third slot 0 or 1 the bid is paired with a blocker and is selected (4.8/N).
8. **Mechanism of the consensus (diag.json, mN 1, Lambda 0, 20 runs).** G1/3 C+B*: of 20 runs 5 end in the core (3 on constants, 2 with a bid in a slot) and 15 on a pair, most of them held by a bid pair or a constant pair; with the single-split menu all 20 are core. At G5/6 every run is a pair, held by constants and bids alike.

*Predictions scored* (spec numbering; full table in `runs/pair-worth.md`): RS conjecture **failed**; RE1 held (core 0.45-0.65 < 0.8, but constants reach the core in about half the runs); RE2 failed; RE3 failed; RE4 failed (no ring in either arm, transfers change nothing); RE5 failed; RE6 held; RE7 failed; RE8 failed; RE9 held by its falsifier (C+A does not exceed C) but "within 0.05" is false (C+A is 0.45 below); RE10 held; RE11 failed in clause (ii) (the blocker is not neutral). Reviewers: sol held (long-lived grand states; chase rate below tail rate); argon failed (core 0.15, coverage mN 0 not all 19). Engineer: E1 held, E2 unresolved, E3 held, E4 failed, E5 held.

*What it means for the program.* (a) The easy-case conjecture is not a dynamic fact under the stated object: the stable-set targets elsewhere remain static menus (as for majority division). The island outcome in G1/3 is a global lottery between the core's equal split and a pair, decided by the founding scramble; bids move it only through the menu (one split: 1.00) or through N (N >= 200). (b) Reading buys nothing the coordination device does not, and costs something: grand constants at the same dose reach the core on 0.80 of islands against 0.25 for bids (bids pair at the exclusion split when the three slots' splits differ), and matching grand constants are selected exactly where bids are, in the excluded slot. (c) The coalitions-map normative claim (dominated outcomes are not where islands end) fails in the middle and hard cases: every middle and hard island ends on a dominated outcome. (d) Both a level-0 handshake and a slot-ordered tie-break are design hazards for any multi-party bid.

*Deviations and caveats.* Level-1 handshake (repair of the spec's bid); V6 literal fails (priority); N = 400 and many secondaries were run (nothing cut); main tail dose Lambda = 10 (queue 71 had not reported); extra controls and 60-run supplement; a fourth process ran for ten minutes. Not run: stage 2 (arm T bids, hook-written bids; gate: C+B core exceeds C by >= 0.2 at N = 100 fails), 24ths stable-set search.

## REJECTED drafts (failed predictions, scored as registered)

- **RS conjecture (easy case): every island in the core, every core point with positive probability.** Failed at the primary arm C+B* (core 0.25 at mN 1; 0.02 core, 78% null at mN 0; 11/19 points ever seen). Holds only for a one-split menu (1.00), i.e. by construction. Re-open only with a bid that coordinates the split without a menu (N-scaling above, or a selection rule).
- **RE1-RE5, RE7, RE8, RE11(ii)** as above. RE2: bids do not lift the easy case at N = 100 (C+B* - C = -0.20). RE3: the tail has no effect at any tested dose. RE4: no ring islands; static grid claim false (13 and 5 undominated at 24ths). RE5: middle and glove end on dominated outcomes. RE7/RE8: no grand state survives migration at a = 5/6; the "neutral blocker then selected second blocker" structure is not the mechanism (a single blocker is selected in slots 0/1). RE11(ii): blocker not neutral.
- **Spec's bid as written** (level-0 handshake): its fallback never fires; the spec's statement that two bids facing a non-bidder pair is false at level 0.
- **Spec V6** (slot-permutation invariance with index-ordered partner priority): fails at 17% of triples.
- **Engineer:** E2 unresolved (level-0 bids vs C), E4 failed (ring islands 0.005 at mN 0, not >= 0.05). Reviewer argon: core >= 0.9 and coverage clauses failed.
- **Theory-tab grid claim** "strict undominated = core proper at a = 2/3 and in the glove market" on 24ths: false (13 and 5 undominated; weak dominance gives the core proper).
