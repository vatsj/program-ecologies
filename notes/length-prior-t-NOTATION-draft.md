- **Length prior over L_T (queue 75):** **R0** — reference machine: the 18 term tags of `lt_code`, a read written out as `(capk k (run k' n p) me)`; **R1** — R0 plus two arity-0 tags `readn`, `readp` (macros for the arm-T reads, cap 400); **branching-probability prior** — each node draws its tag uniformly among the machine's tags, de Bruijn indices and `nat` literals with the Elias-gamma probabilities of n + 1, `con` uniformly among five, `op` among seven, `lib` among 110, so the mass of a term is a product of probabilities and the prior is a semimeasure; **q** — the extinction probability of the branching process, the mass of finite terms (R0 0.4748, R1 2/3); **lost mass** — 1 − q (infinite trees); **s0** — the largest size (nodes) enumerated exhaustively; **junk** — a program that forfeits (non-code output, stuck or out of fuel; charged P_fail) in all probe profiles; **exact member** — a program whose (code, failed reads, forfeit) table over the 29 x 29 probe profiles equals that of a T+ member; **family-level class** — a response-map label on the nine constants (not necessarily reproduced against sampled partners); **rho_R** — the fair single- and two-partner reader mass over the constant mass, exact-member lower bound and Monte Carlo upper bound; **beta** — bully mass over reader mass (the ratio in the prior) or bullies over readers (the dose parameter of series 2a); **delta** — the reader share (single + two) of the seed law, **delta\*(N, I, beta)** — the smallest delta on the grid with mean V\* >= 0.9 at 120N for all larger delta; **c\* = I N delta\*** — founder copies per world at the threshold (**I Lambda delta\*** for the tail); **delta_LP** — the reader share of the prior conditional on finite terms (junk in the denominator); **T+** — arm T's 27 programs plus two Trojan bullies `Bn`, `Bp` (and, in series 2b, the junk programs `Jf` = returns 99, one forfeit, and `Jr` = one failed read then code 8); **series 2a / 2b** — T's constant proportions with (delta, beta) as parameters / the length-prior-shaped law (junk at its measured mass, measured reader ratios).
