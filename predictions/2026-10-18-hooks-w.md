## My predictions (Part W run engineer), with falsifiers and outcome regions

Outcome regions for a paired difference Δ (cell − reference, run-level share of runs or mean over runs): **down** Δ ≤
−0.2, **flat** |Δ| ≤ 0.1, **up** Δ ≥ 0.2, otherwise **grey**; for shares of islands the same thresholds apply to the
mean over runs.

- **W1. Grammar dilution.** The inert control is below the plain baseline in run-level efficiency (all 64 islands
  efficient at the horizon): baseline ≥ 0.95 of runs, inert in [0.3, 0.9]. *Mechanism:* FairBot-like founder mass
  falls about 4× (0.0057 against 0.0226 in the plain grammar), and the per-island establishment chance falls with it.
  *Falsifier:* inert ≥ 0.95 or ≤ 0.1.
- **W2. Contracts make kin cliques.** Under maximal hooks, two default-defecting hook carriers that meet at (D, D)
  contract, and a lineage that meets itself self-contracts; such lineages hold islands as lineage-closed networks.
  Predicted: island efficiency ≥ 0.9 of islands (mean over runs), lineage-closed islands ≥ 0.5 of islands, and
  universal-open ≤ 0.2 of runs (region **down** against both baseline and inert). *Falsifier:* lineage-closed islands
  < 0.2, or universal-open ≥ 0.5.
- **W3. The contract ablation removes them.** With contract fills inert, lineage-closed islands ≤ 0.1 and
  universal-open within 0.15 of inert (**flat**). *Falsifier:* lineage-closed ≥ 0.3 under −contract.
- **W4. The history-scope ablation removes them too** (contract fills are history-scoped): lineage-closed ≤ 0.1.
  *Falsifier:* ≥ 0.3.
- **W5. Held kinds.** At the horizon under maximal, history fills are the most held kind (≥ 0.5 of held fills) and
  behaviour fills ≤ 0.2; RE 1's last clause fails. *Falsifier:* behaviour share ≥ history share.
- **W6. Thanks barely matter.** Grudge-only vs maximal: universal-open and lineage-closed shares **flat**.
  *Falsifier:* |Δ| ≥ 0.2 in either.
- **W7. Seeding-only is near inert.** Seeding-only (written, copied, contract fills inert) has lineage-closed islands
  ≤ 0.05 and universal-open ≥ inert − 0.1. *Mechanism:* seeded history keys are drawn over 6,400 founders, so seeded
  fills almost never act as kin tags; seeded none/role/behaviour fills re-randomize hooks and add a few conditional
  cooperators (founder establisher share 0.0103 vs 0.0057). *Falsifier:* lineage-closed ≥ 0.15 or universal-open
  < inert − 0.2.
- **W8. Recency only changes nothing.** Recency-only vs maximal **flat** on universal-open and island efficiency
  (conflicts between scopes on one tag are rare at ≤ 4 fills). *Falsifier:* |Δ| ≥ 0.2.
- **W9. RE 2 in this wing.** The gentle-holder share at the horizon is small (≤ 0.05 of residents in every cell) and
  its paired change under −writes is not detectable; RE 2's falsifier fires for the weak wing. *Falsifier of W9:* a
  detectable paired change of at least 0.02.
- **W10. Fission–fusion.** Baseline (plain grammar) ≥ 0.9 universal-open and inert ≥ 0.75; under maximal, after the
  merge, one kin lineage or contract network takes the Pangaea, efficient but lineage-closed, so universal-open ≤ 0.3
  (RE 5's falsifier fires). *Falsifier of W10:* maximal fission–fusion universal-open ≥ 0.7.
- **W11. Social organization: threats are tested here.** Worker grudges (strike keyed on the boss's lineage) are
  written on every zero-wage encounter with a hook carrier and fire whenever the boss's lineage recurs: worker threat
  fills are challenged and executed on path at ≥ 10⁻² of the encounters in which they are held (RE 4's execution
  clause fails). *Falsifier:* ≤ 10⁻³.
- **W12. Social organization: the wage.** Under maximal hooks, wage contracts (a boss fill (s, none), s ∈ {1/4, 1/2},
  against a worker's work fill, accepted only as a Pareto improvement for the pair) and worker strike-grudges raise the
  share of islands at a positive wage: fair + intermediate up by ≥ 0.2 against inert, with the fair share up by
  ≥ 0.1 (RE 4's fair clause is grey or fails). *Falsifier:* fair + intermediate within 0.05 of inert.
- **W13. Grammar dilution in the social organization game is small** (inert within 0.1 of the stored baseline on the
  zero-wage share): the union's zero-wage outcome is carried by constants, which dilution does not touch. *Falsifier:*
  |Δ| ≥ 0.2.
