## Scoring conventions (declared now; they do not change the wording above)

- **Primary arm:** S-nonself, constant production r = 100 (amendment 1), founders from one list per replicate
  partitioned into every geometry (amendment 2, notes §1.2). Twenty replicates per cell.
- **Universality.** On a Pangaea: *universal* = the dominant compatible network M is pairwise compatible and holds
  ≥ 0.95 of the population; *universal-open* adds that M is open (mutual with CB-H). For I > 1: the scaling run's
  classifier (universal-open). RE 1 and RE 4 are scored on *universal* (they say "one compatible network"), with
  openness reported beside; RE 3 and RE 6 on universal-open. Notes §1.5 has the definitions.
- **Horizons.** Pangaea predictions are scored at 2,000 generations; RE 3 at 2,000 and at 10,000 (both reported; a
  verdict that differs between them is stated as such). The fission–fusion cell is scored at generation 2,000 counted
  from the start (1,800 generations after the merge at 200; 1,000 after the merge at 1,000).
- **"The TwinD family holds"** = the share of individuals on identity units (TwinD with its certificate); the Q-spelling
  share (certified or not) is reported beside it. "A clique holding ≥ 0.05" is checked at every checkpoint, not only
  at 2,000.
- **All-defect** = *lost* in notes §1.5 (no living cooperator and none reachable by the S-nonself closure).
- **Monotone "beyond run-level uncertainty"**: a decrease between consecutive cells along a total (in increasing N) is
  a violation iff the paired exact McNemar test on the 20 replicates (identical founders) gives two-sided p < 0.05.
  "Crosses 0.5 between (200, 64) and (800, 16)" = (200, 64) ≤ 0.5 ≤ (800, 16) at the 12,800 total; the falsifier and
  the stated crossing are scored separately.
- **The target network** = cooperators mutual with PB-H (PrudentBot's network; FairBot-family holders mutual with PB
  are in it), its share of the whole population.
- **Pass / fail / inconclusive** (amendment 6): a threshold claim "≥ p of runs" passes if the point estimate meets p,
  fails if the exact one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson upper bound is below p, and is inconclusive otherwise; a falsifier
  fires on its own wording. (For 20 runs and p = 0.9: pass ≥ 18, inconclusive 16–17, fail ≤ 15; for p = 0.95: pass
  ≥ 19, inconclusive 17–18, fail ≤ 16; for p = 0.8: pass ≥ 16, inconclusive 13–15, fail ≤ 12.)
- **Counter-predictions.** Sol's is scored clause by clause: (i) the Pangaea at least as universal as (200, 64);
  (ii) strict monotonicity fails or the crossing fails (both failing for "neither"); (iii) all-defect *observed* in at
  least one Pangaea run (otherwise "not observed", which does not refute a possibility claim); (iv) some Pangaea run's
  dominant network is not the target (target share < 0.5). Gemini's: endpoint (b) ≥ 0.95 in ≥ 19/20 Pangaea runs, and
  the raw target share in [0.75, 0.90] in ≥ 5 of 20 runs at 2,000 at a Pangaea.
