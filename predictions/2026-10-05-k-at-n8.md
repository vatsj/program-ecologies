## Design choices fixed before the run (where the spec leaves room)

1. **K is unchanged.** `src/bounded_k.py`'s rules are not touched. Tractability at n = 8 comes from a *GL-erasure
   prune*: every K rule is GL-sound with budgets erased (BoxEq's three cases are GL+Def theorems under a box; Nec is
   necessitation; JLöb is Löb's rule for ∧S), so K ⊢ A implies GL+Def ⊢ erase(A); the free arm's hc/hd tables are
   GL+Def provability (RESULTS "Proof length", audit). Contents whose erasure is not a GL theorem are not searched,
   and JLöb candidate sets S contain only GL theorems. The JLöb candidate set is the reachable box-content closure,
   pruned first and then truncated at 40 elements (at n = 6 the order makes no difference: max |U| = 7 after pruning,
   22 before; tables identical). The largest |U| is reported per budget.
2. **Budgets.** b ∈ {3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 54} for the tables. Chain at b ∈ {4, 8, 16, 54} (b = 16 and 4 first).
3. **Named classes** (in L_8 where present): FairBot, `BOX1(THEM(ME))`, `BOX(THEM(THEM))`, `BOX1(THEM(THEM))`,
   `BOX(THEM(^C))`, `BOX1(THEM(^C))`, PrudentBot `and(BOX(THEM(ME)),BOXD1(THEM(^D)))`, P\*
   `and(BOX1(THEM(ME)),not(BOX(THEM(ME))))`, P2 `and(BOX1(THEM(ME)),not(BOX(THEM(^C))))` (size 9: not in L_8;
   reported from a separate K closure on the named family), the Gödel sentences `not(BOX(THEM(ME)))`,
   `not(BOX(THEM(THEM)))`, the scramble faker `BOX1(THEM(^not(BOX(THEM(ME)))))`. The level-2 ladder (P12b, P\*1b,
   PB2) and Conjecture-4 siblings are out of L_8; their K play is reported from a named-family closure at the same
   budgets, outside the chain.
4. **Leak test** = `proof_length_arm.leak_test`: a self-cooperator's mutual-cooperation component is drift-closed iff
   no member cooperates with some class that defects on it. **Cross-budget catalogue:** every class at every budget
   in the set (constants unbudgeted). Since pairs at one budget play identically inside the catalogue, each fixed-b
   mutual-cooperation graph is an induced subgraph of the catalogue's, every catalogue component is a union of
   fixed-b components plus cross-budget links, and a suckering pair at one budget stays a suckering pair; so if no
   fixed-b component is closed, no catalogue component is closed (a proposition, not a measurement). The catalogue is
   computed only for members of components that are closed at some fixed b (their plays against every class at every
   other budget), which decides whether a fixed-b closure leaks to another budget.
5. **Faker-removal control.** Faker classes are identified operationally: z is a faker if, in the free n = 8 arm, z
   strictly invades (U[z, x] > U[x, x]) a self-cooperating class x that has π ≥ 10⁻³ in the free chain at
   N = 10⁴ or K b = 16 at N = 10⁴, and in K at b = 16 that invasion is gone. Gödel-sentence status (val[z, z] = C with
   no level-0 proof) is reported per faker. The control is the free n = 8 table with those classes deleted from the
   language (μ = 0), nothing else changed. "Share of the gap explained" = (control − free) / (K − free) at N = 10⁴.
6. **Lottery** at n = 8: `almost_all_seeds._run` exactly as `proof_length_arm._lottery_job` (iid seeding from μ,
   mN = 1, W = 0.3), (N, I) = (100, 64), 20 paired seeds per arm (free, K b = 4, K b = 16). Success = every island
   ends held by a cooperator at a self-cooperating class (outcome 'efficient' of `almost_all_seeds.outcome`), censored
   (excluded, counted) if unresolved; Wilson 95%.
7. **Lemma sharing.** (a) *Analytic cut*: the rule Γ ⊢ A, Δ and Γ, A ⊢ Δ / Γ ⊢ Δ (size 1 + premises) with cut formulas
   A ∈ {□B, B : □B a boxed formula reachable from the root by unfolding and subformulas}; minimal (size, Λ) by
   iterative deepening with the exact-value / lower-bound tables (cycles through cut are bounded by the size bound;
   a minimal derivation never repeats a sequent on a branch), oracle-pruned (cut is admissible in GL, so every sequent
   of a derivation is GL-provable), cross-checked against Knuth's algorithm on the full AND-OR graph with cut on every
   n = 6 root. (b) *DAG size*, two variants on each minimal derivation: **exact** (number of distinct sequents; the
   spec's literal measure, the primary one for verdicts) and **subsumption** (a sequent subsumed by an already
   certified sequent — same or larger context on both sides — is a free reference; a lemma-reuse certificate). Both
   are upper bounds on the minimal DAG size over all derivations, stated as such.
8. **Prices** (n = 6, budgets {2, 3, 4, 6, 10, 16} per program, μ split equally, cross-budget plays from K, w = 0.3,
   N = 10⁴): per-match cost on the reader x@b (constants pay nothing): amortized c·b/N; cache c·b·k/N with k the
   number of classes present in the resident/mutant pair state (k = 1 against a copy, 2 otherwise; the chain's
   single-mutant states); lazy c·b against non-constant non-copy opponents with copy = (a) identical budgeted program
   [default], (b) same source at any budget, (c) extensionally identical play (same play row and column in the
   catalogue); per-match c·b. Decisive edges: the top cooperative state's exit edges and its entry edge from all-D;
   per edge Δ = mutant's payoff advantage against the resident, N·Δ, and ρ(mutant)/ρ(neutral) = N·ρ.

   drift-closed component anywhere, or K below free by > 0.05 at b = 54, or the faker-removal control explaining
   < 0.25 of the gap.
3. **P\*-type programs lose their Con-dependent cooperation in K at every budget ≤ 54** (K cannot prove ¬□⊥ → P
   under a box from boxed hypotheses), so P\* self-defects in K. *Falsifier:* P\* self-cooperates in K at some
   b ≤ 54.
4. **Savings are heterogeneous by obligation structure** [after review: sol's reading adopted]: DAG sharing cuts
   PrudentBot from 24 to ≤ 16 while analytic cut alone cuts it by less than DAG sharing does; FairBot stays at 4 under
   every measure; the sibling ratio for PrudentBot stays ≥ 1.5 under DAG sharing. *Falsifier:* PrudentBot ≥ 20
   under DAG sharing, or its sibling ratio < 1.2.
5. **Amortized and cache-accounting prices keep π on cooperating budgets at c ≤ 0.1** (P(C,C) ≥ 0.5 at N = 10⁴,
   π concentrated on budgets 3–4), with the ALLC exit **weakly selected** [after review: sol is right that an
   uncharged ALLC against an incumbent paying c·b/N has N·Δ of order c·b, so the exit is not neutral], fixation
   ratio ρ/ρ_neutral ≤ e^{w·c·b·2} on the decisive edge; **the lazy price locks in a copy-clique at c = 0.1** (one
   budget's identical programs hold ≥ 0.5 of π) **and keeps cooperation at c = 0.01**. *Falsifier:* amortized

   copies holding ≥ 0.4.

## Verdict rules (including predeclared gap verdicts)

- A prediction **held** if every clause held; **failed, falsifier fired** if its falsifier fired; **partly** if a
  non-falsifier clause failed without the falsifier firing (the gap between bound and falsifier), with the failed
  clause named.
- RE 1: K *above* the free arm by more than 0.1 at b ≥ 8 is "partly" (the one-sided falsifier does not fire);
  below by 0.1–0.15 at b = 4 is "partly".
- RE 2: K below free by 0–0.05 at b ≥ 16 is "partly"; a K − free gap < 0.01 at N = 10⁴ makes the faker clause "not
  applicable"; a control share in [0.25, 0.5) is "partly"; a closed component at any budget, or in the catalogue,
  fires the falsifier.
- RE 4: verdicts on the exact-identity DAG (the spec's literal "distinct sequents in the derivation"); if it fails and
  the subsumption DAG passes, the verdict is "split: failed on the literal measure, held on subsumption sharing". The
  sibling ratio is L_read(x → sibling)/L_read(x → x) under the same measure (L_out reported alongside).
- RE 5: verdict per clause (amortized, cache, fixation ratio, lazy c = 0.1, lazy c = 0.01); "π concentrated on
  budgets 3–4" is read as ≥ 0.5 of cooperative π on budgets 3–4 and is not part of the falsifier (gap clause).
- Report "unchanged over the tested budgets", never "saturated". Any cell not run is reported as not run, with the
  prediction left undecided.
