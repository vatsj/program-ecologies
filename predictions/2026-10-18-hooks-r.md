## The subagent's predictions (Part R, PD wing)

Cells: no-hook baseline B (grammar P, world K, the stored cell), inert-hooks control I, maximal M, at (200, 64) to
10,000; ablations at (200, 64) to 2,000 against M's generation-2,000 snapshot; fission–fusion and (400, 64) to 2,000.

- **R1 (gate).** The CHKH library is conservative (every sampled existing check has the same value and step count), the
  gene-site check equals the conflict-rule resolution on every battery case, no accepted certificate has a false atom,
  and Lemma H's reduction equals host replay of the true hooked carriers on every sampled pair. *Falsifier:* any
  difference or any false atom.
- **R2 (seeded keys are nearly inert).** Seeded SELF fills ≤ 0.3 per run (expected ≈ 0.13); among seeded source and
  history fills, the share whose key names a spelling present on the holder's island at generation 0 is ≤ 0.05.
  *Falsifier:* either bound exceeded.
- **R3 (grammar dilution).** UO(I) − UO(B) at 10,000 lies in [−0.2, +0.2]; identity islands in I are 0.
  *Falsifier:* |UO(I) − UO(B)| ≥ 0.3, or any identity island in I.
- **R4 (no seeded identity).** Identity islands ≤ 0.005 per island in M and in every cell whose fills are S-nonself
  (RE 3's first clause fails; gemini's clause holds). *Falsifier:* ≥ 0.02 per island in M.
- **R5 (keyed fills cost universality, not identity).** UO(M) − UO(I) at 10,000 ≤ −0.1 (outcome regions: ≤ −0.1 held;
  (−0.1, 0) inconclusive; ≥ 0 falsifier fired), and M's non-universal runs fail through non-identity rivals.
  *Falsifier:* UO(M) ≥ UO(I).
- **R6 (located in root and behaviour).** Making the root scope inert, or the behaviour scope inert, recovers at least
  half of the M − I gap at 2,000 (each counted separately; held if either does); making source inert changes UO by
  ≤ 0.1. *Falsifier:* neither root-inert nor behaviour-inert exceeds M's UO at 2,000.
- **R7 (history decides little at natural seeds).** |UO(writes inert) − UO(M)| ≤ 0.15 at 2,000, and likewise for the
  shuffled and not-inherited controls. *Falsifier:* any of the three |Δ| ≥ 0.3.
- **R8 (thanks are the most held kind).** In M at 10,000 the history scope holds the largest share of held fills,
  weighted by counts. *Falsifier:* another scope's share exceeds history's by ≥ 0.1.
- **R9 (islands stay efficient).** Mean island P(C,C), excluding whole-run scramble failures, ≥ 0.98 in every cell.
  *Falsifier:* < 0.95 in any cell.
- **R10 (fission–fusion).** UO(M, fission–fusion) ≥ 18/20 at 2,000. *Falsifier:* ≤ 14/20.
- **R11 (the clone route).** In the clone-fills diagnostic, identity islands ≥ 0.02 per island at 2,000 (pair
  contracts between clones mint spelling cliques). *Falsifier:* ≤ 0.005 per island.
- **R12 (other sources add commitments, not losses).** UO(M) − UO(seeding only) ≥ −0.1 at 2,000.
  *Falsifier:* ≤ −0.2.
- **R13 (the conflict rule matters little).** |UO(recency only) − UO(M)| ≤ 0.1 at 2,000. *Falsifier:* ≥ 0.25.
- **R14 (size).** At (400, 64) UO(M) − UO(I) has the sign it has at (200, 64) (or both are 0). *Falsifier:* opposite
  signs with each |Δ| ≥ 0.15.
- **R15 (verification cost).** The modelled median verification cost per encounter in M is ≤ 2× the inert control's.
  *Falsifier:* > 3×.
