# Predictions: island model (conjectures A and B′), 2026-09-23

Written and committed before any experiment cell was run. The only executions so far are the static
computations quoted below (payoff matrices from the cached evaluations, pairwise fixation probabilities,
the migration-game linear programs) and a bookkeeping smoke test of `src/islands.py` on a 4-island toy
and timing runs whose outcomes were not inspected.

## Model (src/islands.py)

- I = 64 islands of N = 100 agents; graph complete or ring. Death uniform; the replacement is a local
  birth (probability 1 − m) or the offspring of a parent drawn by fitness on a uniformly random
  neighbouring island (probability m). Fitness exp(w · mean payoff against the other N − 1 on the
  parent's own island), w = 0.3. mN ∈ {0.1, 1} migrants per island per generation.
- **No mutation.** Every class present at any time was seeded at t = 0.
- Language: weak arm, L_6 with `ROLE` (3,994 programs, 112 classes in PD and in Chicken); Chicken
  without `ROLE`: 1,852 programs, 46 classes.
- Seedings, each placing every program of L_6 at least once (6,400 slots): **programs** (rest uniform
  over programs, shuffled), **prior** (rest from μ, shuffled), **hostile** (rest A_0 = D / Straight,
  shuffled), **clustered** (as programs, dealt in class order so each class fills as few islands as
  possible).
- Horizon 2·10⁵ generations, statistics over the second half; 20 replicates per cell. A run stops early
  when it is **frozen**: all surviving classes pairwise payoff-identical, so no outcome change is possible.
- Games: PD (all four seedings), Chicken with `ROLE` and Chicken without `ROLE` (programs, hostile,
  clustered). Both graphs, both mN.

**Operational reading of the conjectures.** An *island-state* is an island's composition, summarized by its
dominant class (share > ½) and its on-path outcome (P(C,C), mean payoff). *Persistent* island-states are those
occupying islands in the second half of the run, or at freeze. Conjecture A is tested at two levels:
the **outcome** level (the cell-mean second-half P(C,C) or payoff agrees across seedings within 0.1) and the
**class** level (the time-share of each dominant class agrees across seedings within 0.1). Conjecture B′:
falsified by any persistent island-state with positive deadweight loss (THEORY §9.5), measured as a
second-half fraction of island-time in states with DWL > 0.1 of at least 0.1.

## The ε-free object: the migration game

With rare migration on a complete graph, islands are monomorphic between arrivals, and island-state
frequencies x follow replicator dynamics of the antisymmetric game A = ρ − ρᵀ, where ρ[q, a] is the
Moran fixation probability (N = 100, w = 0.3) of one q migrant on an all-a island. Time averages of
replicator dynamics in a zero-sum game converge to its equilibrium set {x : A x ≤ 0}. This is the
island analogue of the per-mutant rates (CLAUDE.md rule 5): it contains no ε and no seeding. It
ignores polymorphic islands, which matters in Chicken without `ROLE`.

Static numbers, PD:
- ρ(`THEM(^C)` into all-D) = 0.042; ρ(`THEM(^D)` into all-`THEM(^C)`) = 0.264; ρ(D into all-ALLC) = 0.264;
  ρ(ALLC into all-`THEM(^C)`) = 0.010 = 1/N (the shadow, neutral); ρ(D into all-`THEM(^C)`) ≈ 0.
- `THEM(^C)` is the only class of L_6 that cooperates with itself and defects against D. It has 15
  strictly advantageous invaders (ρ > 1/N), all of the faker type (they earn more against `THEM(^C)` than it
  earns against itself): `THEM(^D)`, `THEM(^X)`, `THEM(^ROLE)`, `or(ROLE,THEM(^D))`, and so on.
- No class of L_6 is migrant-proof. Every monomorphic state has at least one class that invades it with ρ > 1/N.
- The migration game's equilibrium set is a polytope over 21 classes, **all of them on-path mutual
  defection** (self P(C,C) = 0, payoff −1): `and(X,THEM(^D))`, `and(THEM(THEM),D)`, `and(THEM(ME),D)`, D (up to
  0.50), `THEM(^D)` (up to 0.06), and others. `THEM(^C)` is outside it.

Chicken with `ROLE`: the equilibrium set contains both the `ROLE` conventions (`ROLE`, `not(ROLE)`,
`THEM(^ROLE)`, …; payoff 0.5, the correlated optimum) and self-referential mutual-Swerve states (`THEM(ME)`,
`and(THEM(ME),X)`, …; self-play diverges to the minimax action Swerve, payoff 0). The x-weighted payoff
ranges over [0, 0.5] on the set. The two families exclude each other: ρ(`ROLE` into all-`THEM(ME)`) ≈ 3·10⁻¹⁷,
and ρ(`THEM(ME)` into all-`ROLE`) ≈ 2·10⁻²⁰.

Chicken without `ROLE`: the equilibrium set is 14 classes, all mutual Swerve by divergence (payoff 0, DWL
0.5). The mixed equilibrium (Straight with probability 2/11, payoff −2/11) is not a monomorphic state of
L_6 and is not in the set.

## Verdicts

**1. B′ in the PD: falsified.** In every cell the second-half global P(C,C) (cell mean over replicates) is
below 0.2, and islands with P(C,C) < 0.1 hold at least 0.8 of second-half island-time.
*Falsifier of this prediction:* any cell with mean P(C,C) ≥ 0.5.

**2. The PD spoiler is the faker, not the subsidized front.** This re-opens the REJECTED.md entry "Fakers
as the spatial/island spoiler". The new evidence is structural. That entry rests on fakers sitting at
μ-weight, and μ-weight is a statement about mutation. With migration replacing mutation, μ no longer
weights arrivals. What matters is the invasion structure: the only reciprocator is invaded by 15 faker
classes at ρ up to 0.26, and its shadow, ALLC, is consumed by D during the first within-island selection.
Prediction: among island transitions out of `THEM(^C)` dominance, transitions to a faker-type class (a q
with u(q, `THEM(^C)`) > u(`THEM(^C)`, `THEM(^C)`)) outnumber transitions to an on-path-identical shadow
(ALLC-like) by at least 2 to 1, pooled over cells.
*Falsifier:* shadow exits ≥ faker exits.

**3. ALLC is gone early.** Under the shuffled seedings (programs, prior, hostile) the global ALLC share is
below 0.01 by generation 1,000 and ALLC is extinct by the end in at least 90% of runs. Under the clustered
seeding, the pure-ALLC islands are taken by D migrants (ρ = 0.26). Subsidized fronts exist only in this
transient.

**4. `THEM(^C)` goes extinct.** `THEM(^C)` is extinct by the end of the run in at least half of the
complete-graph runs at mN = 0.1, after which the run freezes into on-path defection.

**5. A in the PD: holds at the outcome level, fails at the class level.** Within each graph × mN, the
seeding means of the second-half P(C,C) lie within 0.1 of each other: every seeding reaches on-path defection.
The composition of the defecting islands differs across seedings, because the equilibrium set is a polytope
(D ranges over [0, 0.50]) and the ε = 0 dynamics cannot leave the face it reaches. Prediction: for some
graph × mN, the time-share of D-dominated islands under the hostile seeding exceeds that under the
programs seeding by more than 0.2.
*Falsifier (outcome level):* two seedings more than 0.2 apart in P(C,C) in the same graph × mN.

**6. B′ in Chicken with `ROLE`: falsified.** Mutual-Swerve islands of the `THEM(ME)` family, at payoff 0
and DWL 0.5, persist alongside `ROLE`-convention islands. In at least one seeding of at least one
graph × mN, at least 0.1 of second-half island-time is spent in states with payoff ≤ 0.1.
*Falsifier:* every cell's mean payoff ≥ 0.45, meaning every island reaches a `ROLE` convention.

**7. A in Chicken with `ROLE`: fails at the outcome level.** The split between convention islands and
mutual-Swerve islands is set by the seeding and frozen by mutual exclusion (ρ ≈ 10⁻¹⁷ both ways). The
cell-mean payoff differs by more than 0.1 between two seedings in at least one graph × mN.
*Falsifier:* all seedings within 0.1 in every graph × mN.

**8. Chicken without `ROLE`: the B′ falsifier fires, but not at the mixed equilibrium.** Second-half mean
payoff lies in [−0.1, 0.1] with P(Swerve, Swerve) ≥ 0.8. These are self-referential programs whose
self-play diverges to the minimax action. The mixed equilibrium would show payoff −0.18 and
P(Swerve, Swerve) ≈ 0.67. THEORY §9.5 predicts "persisting at the mixed equilibrium", and that prediction
is refuted if this verdict holds.
*Falsifier of this verdict:* payoff ≤ −0.12 (the mixed equilibrium) or ≥ 0.3.

**9. Graph and migration rate do not change the verdicts.** The ring and mN = 1 give the same verdicts as
complete mN = 0.1 on items 1, 6 and 8. No quantitative prediction is made about their size.

## What each outcome would mean

- If 1 holds: the island model does not rescue the dilemma. Removing μ-injection removes the shadow's
  class-size advantage, but it also removes the reciprocator's only route back after a faker takes its
  island. The PD failure is then not specific to mutation.
- If 2 fails, meaning the shadow-then-D route dominates: the unconditional shadow is the obstruction even
  without mutation, via the seeded ALLC, and THEORY's predicted spoiler stands.
- If 6 and 7 hold: B′ as stated ("every persistent island-state") is false even in coordination games
  with `ROLE` at ε = 0. The chain's efficiency in Chicken would then rest on mutation supplying the
  transitions between mutually exclusive conventions.
