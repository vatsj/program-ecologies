## Design choices fixed before the run (where the spec leaves room)

1. **Two forms of the rule.** *K+4* is the spec's literal rule (`four='lit'`). *K+4m* (`four='mono'`) is the literal
   rule closed under the monotonicity K's BoxEq already has at top level and under one unfolding (notes §1.2, cases
   (i)–(iv)); it is sound by the same proof. By Lemma V the literal tables are a regression check (computed at n = 6,
   b = 4, 16, 40 and n = 8, b = 16, 54). **The counted arm for Part A (tables at every spec budget, sweeps, chain,
   lottery) and for the K+4 label of Part B and Part C is K+4m**, the strongest sound reading of "a 4-rule for K";
   the RE's predictions get a verdict under each form.
2. **Exploratory X-arm (not in the spec):** the guard read one budget up, BOXk at budget b reading
   □_b(¬□_{b+1}^k⊥ → s) (`goff = 1`), under K and K+4m. Motivation: Lemma G. Computed for the named-family sweep
   (b = 4…40) and the n = 8 tables at b = 16 and 54; chain at b = 16 at N = 10⁴ only if time allows. It changes the
   programs' semantics, so it is reported apart from every RE verdict.
3. **Tables.** n = 6 at b ∈ {4, 16, 40}; n = 8 at b ∈ {4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 54}, with the GL-erasure prune (valid for
   K+4m, notes §1.4 remark (b)) and `src/k_at_n8.py`'s JLöb candidate rule. Soundness: the plain checker on every
   table, plus the closure checker (`closure_check`, every box content of a flagged formula solved) on anything the
   plain checker flags. Cross-budget catalogue at n = 8 over b ∈ {4, 16} under K+4m (Lemma V′: the place where 4m can
   act on DSL programs without a JLöb hypothesis).
4. **Dense sweeps.** A named-family K closure (trace-pruned, as `k_at_n8.family_table`) at every b from 4 to 40 for:
   PB2, P\*, P2, P12b, P\*1b, PrudentBot, FairBot, `BOX1(THEM(ME))`, the 21 Gödel-sentence fakers of "K at n = 8", their
   free-arm victims among the supported self-cooperators, C and D; under K, K+4m and the X-arm. Reported per program:
   the first b at which it self-cooperates (b\*) and the certified witness cost at b\*, i.e. the exact minimal K(+4m)
   sizes T of its atoms' contents (the fixed point's values, each witnessed by a derivation). A Gödel sentence is
   **re-armed** at b if it strictly invades one of its free-arm victims in the family table at b.
5. **Tests.** Nested boxes (□_a A ⊢ □_c □_d A at every (a, c, d) ≤ 8 against the case table), cross-budget boundaries
   (c = a, a + 1, a + 2 and d = a − 1, a, a + 1), and 300 generated formulas outside the catalogue (random formulas
   over FairBot, PrudentBot, a Gödel sentence, C and D at budgets 2–8, up to box depth 3) with the closure checker.
6. **Chain** (n = 8, w = 0.3, PD): K+4m at b = 16 and 54, N = 10³, 10⁴, 3·10⁴, against K and the free arm (published
   rows of "K at n = 8"). The canonical classes of L_8 carry the source-level mass (`L.mu_canon`, the aggregate of
   their sources) in every arm; behavioural classes are recomputed per arm, and splits and merges against K are listed
   with their aggregate μ. The K+4m faker set is defined as for K (strict invaders of a supported self-cooperator whose
   invasion K+4m removes, against free); **restored cooperators** = classes self-cooperating in K+4m and not in K;
   **restored exploiters** = classes strictly invading a supported self-cooperator in K+4m and not in K. Dominant entry
   and exit: the top cooperative state's largest exit and the all-D → top-state entry, with N·ρ and the N-slope of the
   exit weight over the three N.
7. **Classifier, frozen** (code in `src/k_four.py`, committed with this file; no edit after this commit).
   *C_spec (primary):* for an eligible pair (z, x), the minimal (size, Λ) cut-free GLS+Def derivation of z's free-arm
   play against x at the lowest level k ∈ {0, 1, 2} at which it is provable; flagged iff some GLR application has
   (G) a Gödel-sentence premise, read as: a boxed formula □B on the left of the GLR premise, *used* in that premise's
   subtree (it, or B, is principal or closes an initial sequent there), with B ∈ {P_uu, φ(P_uu), ¬P_uu, ¬φ(P_uu)}
   for some program u, of the polarity of u's free-arm self-play, and ⊢ B not GL-provable (Oracle); or (C) a Con
   statement under a box: a used formula of the GLR premise (or its goal) with a subformula □C whose C contains a
   guard ¬□^k⊥ (k ≥ 1). The spec's "¬B is the program's own play constant" is read as "B is the true literal of a
   program's self-play (up to one unfolding)", i.e. K-at-n8's Gödel status (self-play true at the stable world, no
   level-0 proof), generalized to either polarity. Uncertified minimal searches (2·10⁶ expansions) fall back to the
   oracle's greedy derivation and are counted. *C_pair (secondary, descriptive):* flagged if either play of the
   invasion (z vs x, x vs z) is flagged.
   *Eligible catalogue:* (z, x) with x in the 25 supported self-cooperators of "K at n = 8" (`runs/k-at-n8-fakers.json`)
   and U_free[z, x] > U_free[x, x]. *Labels:* K-disarmed = U_K[z, x] ≤ U_K[x, x] at b = 16; K+4m-disarmed likewise.
   Positive = disarmed. Precision = P(disarmed | flagged), recall = P(flagged | disarmed), at the pair level
   (primary) and the class level (a class is flagged / disarmed if any of its eligible pairs is). *Held-out:* the
   n = 6 catalogue, residents = self-cooperators with π ≥ 10⁻³ in the n = 6 free or K b = 16 chain at N = 10⁴, label
   K-disarmed at b = 16.
   *Misclassified pairs:* for each, the GL-true atoms among the three plays that decide the invasion (z vs x, x vs z,
   x vs x) that are K-false at b = 16; each is **structural** if not K-derivable at any budget ≤ 2·L + 10 (L = its
   cut-free GLS+Def minimal size) and **finite-budget** otherwise (closure on the pair's family at cap 2L + 10); and
   whether a GL derivation of z's play without any triggered GLR exists up to twice the minimal size (restricted exact
   search).
8. **Part C** (free chain at n = 8, N = 10⁴; each against K b = 16, K+4m b = 16 and free): (i) the free table with
   the C_spec-flagged invader classes deleted (μ = 0; class-level flag); (ii) the K b = 16 table with the restored
   cooperators' rows and columns taken from K+4m b = 16; (iii) the K b = 16 table with the restored exploiters' rows
   and columns taken from K+4m. "Recovered share" of (i) = (P(C,C)(i) − free)/(K − free).
9. **Lottery:** `k_at_n8.lottery_job` unchanged, K+4m b = 16, (100, 64), mN = 1, the same 20 seeds as "K at n = 8".
10. **≤ 3 workers.** Priority as in the spec; anything not run is reported as not run.

## RE predictions (verbatim from the spec)

1. **K+4 is sound** (the inductive proof goes through; 0 violations) **and re-arms PB2 and the level-2 ladder at
   some b ≤ 54; whether P\* and P2 return is left open** (sol's point: soundness does not forbid proving a bounded
   or weaker consistency, and the RE has not identified the exact Con statement they need; the dense sweep decides).
   *Falsifier:* a soundness violation or a gap in the proof that cannot be closed, or PB2 still self-defecting at
   b = 54.
2. **Re-arming the cooperators also changes invasion targets, and the sign of K+4 − K is not predictable from faker
   counts** (sol's reading adopted); the RE's guess is K+4 between the free arm and K at N = 10⁴, with at most 5 of
   the 21 Gödel sentences re-armed. *Falsifier:* K+4 above K by ≥ 0.03 or below the free arm by ≥ 0.03 at N = 10⁴,
   or ≥ 10 Gödel sentences re-armed. Grey zone (between): inconclusive, predeclared.
3. **The frozen classifier is an enrichment, not a characterization:** precision ≥ 0.7 and recall ≥ 0.7 on the
   eligible catalogue for the K-disarmed label, with counterexamples to an iff in both directions (finite-budget
   failures without a trigger; triggered derivations with an alternative untriggered one). *Falsifier:* precision or
   recall < 0.5. Grey zone 0.5–0.7: inconclusive.
4. **K+4 closes nothing** at any budget or across budgets; lottery ≥ 18/20 (17: inconclusive; ≤ 16: falsified).
5. **Part C:** deleting only the classifier-flagged classes from the free table recovers ≥ 0.5 of the K − free gap
   at N = 10⁴; restoring K+4's exploiting plays alone lowers P(C,C) relative to K by more than restoring its
   cooperating plays alone raises it. *Falsifier:* the flagged-deletion recovers < 0.25 of the gap.

## Subagent predictions (with falsifiers)

- **S1. Literal K+4 = K** at every computed cell (n = 6, b = 4, 16, 40; n = 8, b = 16, 54), by Lemma V.
  *Falsifier:* any play differs (an implementation error or a gap in Lemma V).
- **S2. K+4m re-arms none of PB2, P\*, P2, P12b, P\*1b** at any b from 4 to 40 in the family sweep, nor at b = 54 in
  the n = 8 named cells (Lemma G for PB2; P\*, P2 and the Gödel sentences need distribution under a box).
  *Falsifier:* any of the five self-cooperates in K+4m at some b ≤ 54.
- **S3. K+4m barely moves the n = 8 tables:** at b ≥ 16 at most 1% of the 372,100 plays differ from K, and
  |P(C,C)(K+4m) − P(C,C)(K)| ≤ 0.01 at N = 10⁴ (b = 16 and 54). *Falsifier:* > 1% of plays, or a gap > 0.01.
- **S4. No Gödel sentence is re-armed** in K+4m at any b from 4 to 40 (0 of 21). *Falsifier:* ≥ 1.
- **S5. X-arm:** with the guard read one budget up, K+4m re-arms PB2 at some b ≤ 40 and K does not (at any b ≤ 40);
  P\* and P2 stay disarmed under both. *Falsifier:* PB2 not re-armed under K+4m/g+1 by b = 40, or re-armed under
  K/g+1, or P\* or P2 re-armed under either.
- **S6. Classifier on the K label (pair level):** recall ≥ 0.8 and precision ≥ 0.6. Most disarmed pairs are Gödel
  sentences or readers of Gödel sentences whose D-play derivation uses □P_GG as a hypothesis. *Falsifier:*
  recall < 0.6 or precision < 0.4.
- **S7. Held-out n = 6:** C_spec flags every K-disarmed n = 6 pair (recall 1.0). *Falsifier:* it misses one.
- **S8. Misclassifications:** every K-disarmed pair's lost atoms are structural (none K-derivable within 2L + 10).
  *Falsifier:* any finite-budget failure among the K-disarmed pairs.
- **S9. Part C (i)** recovers between 0.8 and 1.5 of the K − free gap. *Falsifier:* outside [0.5, 2].
- **S10. Part C (ii) and (iii)** are each within 0.005 of K b = 16 at N = 10⁴ (S3: little is restored).
  *Falsifier:* either differs from K by > 0.01.
- **S11. K+4m chain:** top exits neutral into ALLC with exit slope in [−1.05, −0.95], no strict top exit, the
  all-D → FairBot entry N·ρ = 43.5 as in K, and nothing closed at any fixed b or on the {4, 16} catalogue.
  *Falsifier:* any of these.
- **S12. Lottery** K+4m b = 16: 20/20. *Falsifier:* ≤ 18/20.

## Verdict rules (including predeclared gap verdicts)

- A prediction **held** if every clause held; **failed, falsifier fired** if its falsifier fired; **partly** if a
  non-falsifier clause failed without the falsifier firing, the failed clause named; **inconclusive** in a
  predeclared grey zone.
- **RE 1** is decided under each form (literal K+4, K+4m). Soundness is the proof in notes §1 plus 0 violations. PB2
  self-defecting at b = 54 fires the falsifier; PB2 re-armed but some ladder member (P12b, P\*1b) not re-armed at
  b ≤ 54 is "partly"; the P\*/P2 clause is open and only reported. The X-arm does not enter RE 1's verdict.
- **RE 2** (per form): K+4 − K ≥ +0.03 or free − K+4 ≥ 0.03 at N = 10⁴ (b = 16), or ≥ 10 of 21 Gödel sentences
  re-armed at some b ≤ 54: falsifier fired. K+4 in [free − 0.005, K + 0.005] with ≤ 5 Gödel sentences re-armed:
  the guess held. Anything else (above K by 0.005–0.03, below free by 0.005–0.03, or 6–9 re-armed): inconclusive
  (the spec's grey zone). Its first clause (re-arming changes invasion targets) is "not tested" if nothing is
  re-armed.
- **RE 3:** pair-level C_spec on the K label. Precision and recall ≥ 0.7 and a counterexample in each direction:
  held; either < 0.5: falsifier fired; otherwise inconclusive (grey zone 0.5–0.7), or "partly" if both ≥ 0.7 and a
  direction lacks a counterexample.
- **RE 4:** a closed component at any fixed b or on the catalogue fires it; lottery 17/20 inconclusive, ≤ 16/20
  fires it.
- **RE 5:** recovered share ≥ 0.5: first clause held; < 0.25: falsifier fired; 0.25–0.5: inconclusive. The second
  clause is decided only if (ii) or (iii) moves P(C,C) by more than 0.001 from K; otherwise "not tested".
- Report "unchanged over the tested budgets", never "saturated". A cell not run is reported as not run and its
  prediction left undecided.
