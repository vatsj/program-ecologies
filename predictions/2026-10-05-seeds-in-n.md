## Definitions fixed before the run

- *Per-island chance p(N, n):* fraction of islands in the no-migration control (100 runs × 4 islands per cell) that end
  efficient (island P(C,C) ≥ 0.95, locally frozen), Wilson 95%.
- *Certified cooperative island:* every present class is a self-cooperator and the present classes are pairwise
  payoff-identical; *certified core island:* the same with every present class unfakeable.
- *Loss mechanism:* for every island that was ≥ 90% cooperative and fell below 50%, the composition at the last check
  (every 20 generations) at which it was ≥ 90% cooperative is logged (top 6 classes and the number present). With q the
  largest class after the loss and a the largest resident other than q: if a was the only resident, the loss is a
  *strict invasion of a monomorphic island* when U(q, a) > U(a, a), a *neutral replacement* when equal, a *fixation
  against selection* when less; otherwise it is *displacement from a mixed island*. A loss is core-held when a is
  unfakeable.
- *μ × establishment (prediction 4):* E_k = μ_k · ρ_k(D, N), the exact fixation probability of one k on an all-D island
  of size N. Rank correlation (Spearman) of frozen-efficient island shares (fraction of islands whose largest class is k,
  over efficient runs) against E_k, over the self-cooperators.
- *Predictor (prediction 1):* p(N, n) ≈ a·μ_core(n)·√N, a fitted on p(100, 6) alone (as the spec states) and, as a
  second fit, on N = 100, 400, 1,600 at n = 6; residuals at n = 7–9 reported for both.
- *Island-level ALLC extinction:* exact time (birth-level) at which each island's ALLC count first reaches 0; the
  global time is reported separately.
- Outcome of a prediction: **held** if the claimed quantity's interval lies inside the claim, **failed** if it lies
  outside or a falsifier fires, **inconclusive** if intervals overlap the boundary or each other where the claim is a
  comparison.

## RE predictions (Fable), carried from the spec

1. **One step down, then flat.** The per-island chance p(100, n) is 0.07–0.08 at n = 6 and 0.045–0.06 at n = 7, 8, 9,
   tracking μ_core (ratio 0.69) to within ±25%; it does not fall further from 7 to 9. *Falsifier:* the Wilson interval
   of p(100, 9) entirely below 0.03, or the n = 7 and n = 9 intervals disjoint with n = 9 lower. Overlapping intervals
   are inconclusive. Sol's alternative: flat outcomes could reflect FairBot's flat prior alone rather than the whole
   core; the composition in 4 separates these.
2. **Run-level fractions approximately follow 1 − (1 − p)^I** (approximate, since no-migration p measures
   establishment without migration and spread is assumed; the nucleation-then-spread control tests both parts). At
   I = 4 the efficient fraction at n ≥ 7 is 0.15–0.25 (N = 100), 0.30–0.45 (400), 0.55–0.75 (1,600); at I = 64 and 256
   the point estimate is at least 0.95 at every n (40 of 40 gives a Wilson lower bound of 0.91, which is the resolvable
   claim). *Falsifier:* (100, 256) with a Wilson upper bound below 0.9 at any n, or (1,600, 4) with an upper bound below
   0.45 at n = 9.
3. **Post-ALLC losses are fakeable provers taken by probe-fakers; core losses, if any, are not strict invasions.**
   Islands lost after ALLC extinction are, at least 0.9 of them, held by fakeable self-cooperators and taken by
   probe-fakers; any loss of a core-held island is reconstructed as neutral or mixed-state displacement, never as a
   strict invasion; and the number of post-ALLC losses grows with n. Runs with losses still freeze efficient in at least
   0.95 of cases. *Falsifier:* a reconstructed strict invasion of a monomorphic core island, which would contradict
   soundness.
4. **The frozen efficient states track prior mass × establishment, not description length as such.** FairBot and
   `BOX1(THEM(ME))`, which carry most of the core's μ at every n and have the cheapest entry, hold at least 0.6 of the
   islands in frozen efficient states at every n; the share held by fakeable provers falls with n. Report island shares
   against μ × establishment probability per class. *Falsifier:* fakeable provers holding more than 0.3 of frozen
   efficient islands at n = 9, or island shares uncorrelated with μ × establishment (rank correlation below 0.3).
5. **Island-level ALLC extinction is n-independent:** the island-level median extinction generation lies between 15
   and 60 at every n and N, with the distribution at n = 9 within a factor of 1.5 of n = 6 at each N; the global time
   grows with I as the maximum over islands. *Falsifier:* island-level median above 120 at any n.

## Subagent's predictions (written after the static facts above, before any island run)

- **S1. No step at n = 7.** The n = 6 → 7 drop in μ_core is `BOX(THEM(THEM))` being reclassified as fakeable by fakers
  of mass ~10⁻⁵, which an island almost never seeds. So p(100, n) does not drop by the core ratio: the point estimates
  at n = 7, 8, 9 are within ±25% of p(100, 6), and the n = 7 point estimate is above 0.06. (This contradicts the range
  in RE 1, not its falsifier.) *Falsifier:* p(100, 7) and p(100, 6) intervals disjoint with n = 7 lower.
- **S2. The right predictor is establishment-weighted cooperative mass, not μ_core.** Fitting a·μ·√N on p(100, 6)
  with μ = μ_est(n), the prior mass of D-entering self-cooperators (0.0229–0.0246), leaves residuals at n = 7–9 of
  at most ±25% at every N; fitting with μ_core leaves residuals of +30% or more at n = 7–9 (point estimates).
  *Falsifier:* the μ_core fit has smaller absolute residuals at n = 7–9 than the D-entering-mass fit.
- **S3. `BOX(THEM(THEM))` and `BOX1(THEM(THEM))` hold frozen efficient islands at n ≥ 7 as often as at n = 6**, so
  the fakeable share does not fall with n (contradicting part of RE 4): their combined share of frozen efficient islands
  at n = 9 is within the n = 6 interval.
- **S4. Post-ALLC loss counts are not resolvably different across n**: the probe-fakers' mass grows by ~20% from n = 6
  to n = 9, so the loss count per run at n = 9 is within a factor of 1.5 of n = 6 and the "grows with n" clause of RE 3
  will be inconclusive.
