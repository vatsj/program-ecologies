## The RS's statement (2026-10-23, quoted in the spec)

"I suspect we're gonna need strong notions of mixed actions to recover equilibria in adversarial games. I propose rock
paper scissors for starters? We should recover the mixed-strategy equilibrium pretty straightforwardly."

The three readings of "recover the mixed equilibrium" in the spec: (W) the population's time-averaged play is uniform;
(M) the population is a mixture of pure programs that is uniform at each time; (I) individual programs mix and the
programs that hold the population are unexploitable. The RS's statement is scored on (I) ("strong notion"), with (W) and
(M) reported: it holds if, in the stochastic arms at N = 10^4 (chain) and on the lottery, the population's
in-population program exploitability is at most 0.05 and the individual-mixing share is at least 0.8. It fails if
exploitability exceeds 0.15 or mixing falls below 0.5 in every stochastic arm; partial otherwise.

## Scoring conventions fixed here (engineer, before any code), so no threshold can move afterwards

* **Cells.** Grammar nodes n = 6 (n = 7 only if the budget allows, disclosed), D = 12, w = 0.3, prior mu(p) = 2^-bits with
  bits = log2 a(|p|) + 2 log2 |p| + 1 (the weak arm's length prior, uniform inside a length class; IMPLEMENTATION §2 forbids
  2^-nodes as improper; the spec's "2^-nodes" is read as this convention, and 2^-nodes is run as a labelled chain
  sensitivity). Chain: N in {100, 1000, 10^4}, LogChain, closure-seeded exploration with theta_log <= 1e-14 and the 1e-12
  and 1e-16 sensitivities, twin-expanded distribution beside the lumped one. Lottery: epsilon = 0, iid founders from mu
  (common random numbers across arms: the same slot uniforms are mapped through each arm's enumeration; exact versus
  sample-matched are paired spelling for spelling), complete island graph, (N, I) = (100, 64), mN = 1 (m = 1/N), w = 0.3,
  40 runs, horizon 2,000 generations of I*N events, clustered intervals by run (bootstrap over runs, 4,000 resamples).
* **Exploitability.** The primary exploitability is the **in-population program exploitability**
  e(x) = max_k (U x)_k over all classes k of the arm's enumerated grammar (U antisymmetric, so the population earns 0).
  Chain: pi-weighted over recurrent states. Lottery: per island at the horizon, then over islands, then over runs.
  **Action exploitability** (the best expected payoff of a constant action against the population's play marginal,
  forfeit counted as a win for the constant), the program exploitability e(p) of single classes and the residents'
  unilateral program-deviation regret are reported beside it.
* **Individual mixing.** The share of mass (pi, or lottery population) of programs whose distribution over
  (R, P, S, forfeit), against a random member of the population, is within 0.05 total variation of (1/3, 1/3, 1/3, 0).
  The same against itself is reported. Forfeit mass counts as a deviation from uniform.
* **Self-play forfeit share.** pi- (or population-) weighted sum of x_a * P(a forfeits against a). The plays-in-population
  forfeit share is reported beside it.
* **Constants** are the three classes of R, P, S; **X** is the class of X; **guarded readers** are the classes of
  `coin X (next(THEM(ME)))` and `coin X (cr(THEM(ME)))` (each is reported, and for the sample arms the former only).
* Predictions with a conjunction are scored partial when one part holds; "indeterminate" when the evidence cannot decide.
* Cells that fail to complete are reported as such; no verdict is scored on an incomplete cell.

## Predictions (the RE's, verbatim from the spec; falsifiers in brackets)

1. *Statics.* Against `X` every program earns exactly 0 unless it forfeits [any non-forfeiting program earns ≠ 0
   against `X`]. `cr(THEM(ME))` against itself forfeits [it terminates with positive probability]. `next(THEM(ME))`
   beats every constant and every program that terminates against it deterministically [a counterexample].
   *Scoring:* checked on every program of the n = 6 table in the sample and exact arms (tolerance 1e-9). "Terminates
   against it deterministically" means G_12(b, N) is a point mass on one of R, P, S; the claim is u(N, b) = +1.
2. *(W) is trivial as an ensemble statement.* Relabeling equivariance of the payoff tensor and of π holds to 10⁻⁹
   [any violation: an implementation bug]. [Revised after second review] Symmetry fixes the ensemble, not each
   trajectory: pooled lottery action frequencies are uniform within run-clustered intervals, and per-run asymmetry
   (non-ergodic lotteries can break symmetry within a run) is reported, not predicted.
   *Scoring:* equivariance of U for every arm (cyclic relabeling of programs, classes mapped through the permutation) and
   of pi for every chain cell (state mapped through the class permutation); lottery: pooled R, P, S frequencies over
   non-forfeit plays, each within its run-clustered 95% interval of 1/3.
3. *Pure arm.* The holders are simulating readers whose self-play forfeits, not a mixture: π-weighted forfeit share
   of self-play ≥ 0.5 and π mass of constants ≤ 0.2 at N = 10⁴; individual mixing is impossible (no coin), so the arm
   "recovers" the game's value through mutual non-termination, not the equilibrium [constants hold ≥ 0.5 of π].
   *Counter-prediction (gemini-4-argon), registered:* the depth cap creates a ladder in the pure arm (shallower
   readers beat deeper ones at the boundary), or readers tie at 0 and drift neutrally while constants cycle.
   *Scoring:* held if both inequalities hold at N = 10^4; failed if constants hold >= 0.5 of pi (the falsifier); partial
   if the forfeit share is below 0.5 or the constants' mass exceeds 0.2 without reaching 0.5. Counter-prediction scored as
   (L) ladder: the pi-weighted fraction of ordered pairs of distinct support classes whose payoff sign differs between
   D = 11, 12, 13, 20 exceeds 0.05; (N) neutral drift: the support's readers are mutually payoff-neutral (all pairwise
   payoffs among reader classes in the support equal 0 at D = 12) and the constants' pi mass is >= 0.2.
4. *Sample arm.* Individual mixing appears but readers exploit non-uniform mixers only partially, so π-weighted
   exploitability is positive and falls with N more slowly than in the exact arm [sample-arm exploitability at most
   the exact arm's at every N].
   *Scoring:* held if sample-arm exploitability exceeds exact-arm exploitability at all three N and its ratio
   e(10^4)/e(100) is larger; failed if the sample arm's exploitability is at most the exact arm's at every N (the
   falsifier); partial otherwise. "Positive" means above 0.005 at N = 10^4.
5. *Exact arm: selection for individual mixing.* At N = 10⁴, π-weighted exploitability ≤ 0.05 and individual mixing
   ≥ 0.8 of π mass [exploitability > 0.15 or mixing < 0.5]. In the lottery, at least 0.8 of islands have
   in-population exploitability ≤ 0.05 [below 0.5]. [After review] Exact reading itself does the work: exact −
   sample-matched exploitability is negative at N = 10⁴ [≥ 0]. *Counter-prediction (gemini-4-argon), registered:*
   `X` (one node) holds most of the mixing mass in both arms as a neutral shadow, so exact beats sample only through
   the reader prior mass, and the matched control erases the difference. *Counter-prediction (gpt-6.1-sol),
   registered:* `X` already supplies minimax protection in both stochastic arms and readers pay recursive-forfeit
   exposure, so mixing is substantial in both and exact − matched has no robust sign until truncation is controlled.
   *Scoring:* three sub-claims, (5a) chain exploitability <= 0.05 and mixing >= 0.8 at N = 10^4, (5b) lottery island share
   with exploitability <= 0.05 at least 0.8 (pooled over runs, clustered interval reported), (5c) exact minus
   sample-matched exploitability negative at N = 10^4 (chain, point estimate, and the lottery island-mean difference with
   its paired run-clustered interval reported). Held if (5a)-(5c) all hold; failed if exploitability > 0.15 or mixing
   < 0.5 at N = 10^4 (the chain falsifier), or the lottery share is below 0.5, or (5c) is >= 0; partial if the claims
   split between hold and the falsifier region. Counter-prediction (gemini) scored as: pi(X) is at least 0.5 of the mixing
   mass in both exact and sample at N = 10^4 and |exact - matched| <= 0.02. Counter-prediction (gpt) scored as: individual
   mixing >= 0.5 in both sample and exact at N = 10^4, the exact - matched difference lies within 0.02, and its sign differs
   across D in {11, 12, 13, 20} or across N in {100, 1000, 10^4}.
6. *Uniform default.* In exact-uniform, forfeits vanish and the readers' self-play becomes uniform, so the pure arm's
   loophole and the exact arm's mixing become indistinguishable in play; reported as a check on the forfeit rule's
   role [no change against exact].
   *Scoring (reading fixed here):* the prediction is that the forfeit rule matters. Held if exact-uniform has plays-forfeit
   share < 1e-6 and differs from exact by more than 0.05 in at least one of the pi-weighted exploitability, the individual-
   mixing share, or the pi mass of the exact arm's top-3 support classes; failed ("no change against exact") if forfeits
   vanish and every one of these three is within 0.02 of exact; partial otherwise. The "indistinguishable in play" claim
   is reported separately: the total variation of the reader-heavy support states' self-play action distribution from
   uniform, at most 0.05.

The RE's confidence: 1 and 2 high; 3 medium (depends on which readers terminate); 4 low; 5 medium.
