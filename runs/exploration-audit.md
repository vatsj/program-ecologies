## Summary

  states, 15 with the monomorphic space itself restricted); 7 complete or not chains. **15 groups re-solved at stage (b) or later; 30
  unresolved** (reasons below; I-49 was reproduced but its enlargement did not finish).
* **Controls.** Positive control (mixed2 inspection exact, c = 0, N = 10^4) reproduces both published numbers: -0.0093 at the published
  1,500-state cap (published -0.009) and -0.4707 on the whole 24,649-state space (published -0.471); the 4,000-state point does not
  reproduce (-0.4708 here against -0.31 in `notes/mixed2.md`); the statistic has converged from 4,000 states although the cap is still binding
  at 15,000 (frontier mass 0.52, 1,150 candidates left). Negative control (blind inspection, 289 states, complete): capped and whole-space
  values identical. Caps and exploration seeds on the sample arm (6,889 states): cap 500 gives -0.947, 1,500 gives -0.643 with the published
  seeds and -0.644 / -0.644 / -0.645 with three random seed sets, 3,000 gives -0.6424 = the whole space. Solver check: the jump-chain GTH agrees with a
  dense continuous-time generator solve to TV 1.2·10^-15 (blind) and 2.8·10^-14 (sample), and with the log-domain GTH to 7.7·10^-16 (blind), on identical
  state sets and rates.
* **What moved.** Only cells whose stored published record shows a cap hit or an unfinished round: the one-population modal hawk–dove hd3
  chain (payoff per player 0.234 → 0.906 at N = 10^3, still capped; 0.016 → 0.183 at N = 10^4, converged under the enlargement) and, as
  uncited cells, mixed2 sr16 (-2.456 → -0.361) and sr4 (-0.764 → -0.352), both still capped at 15,000 states. Every re-solved cell without
  a stored cap hit moved by at most 0.004 on the spec's scale.
* **What did not move** (threshold 10 to 10^4 times lower, or cap 2 to 10 times larger, or the whole space): the ultimatum arms (≤ 0.0003),
  the modal fixed-role dollar (≤ 0.0005; 8,000 → 16,000 of 42,025 states), the union game N = 10^4 (≤ 0.003), enforcement RR / RC (≤ 2·10^-5),
  three-player weak N = 100 (≤ 0.0005), the modal / K-arm PD cells at n = 8 (≤ 0.0004), modal dollar N = 100 and 3·10^4, the weak dollar
  comparator (twin-expanded), mixed2 exact c = 0.01 N = 100 on the whole space (-0.7920 vs -0.7921 capped), and the concessions P01 fair
  share, which falls from 0.0086 to 0.0050 as theta drops 3.3 times (toward the published cut-corrected 0.0020).

## For the research supervisor (plain words)

1. **The failure mode is real but narrow.** Of 45 chain results with restricted exploration, the published numbers that moved are exactly those whose own stored records
   already showed a cap being hit: one cell family of the fixed-role chicken / hawk–dove section and two mixed2 side cells that RESULTS already marks "not converged". Every
   other cell that could be re-solved (ultimatum, union, enforcement, three-player, modal and K-arm PD, the modal dollar and its fixed-role chain, whole-space mixed2)
   agrees with its published value to four digits when the threshold is 10 to 10^4 times lower or the cap 2 to 10 times larger.
2. **One sentence needs a decision.** The one-population modal hawk–dove (hd3) chain was at its 3,000-state cap. With 12,000 states, hd3 gives payoff per player 0.18 (not 0.02) at
   N = 10^4, and 0.91 (not 0.23, still capped) at N = 10^3: one population with no roles then reaches about nine tenths of the fixed-role surplus at N = 10^3. "One population
   cannot learn the norm" holds at N = 10^4 and for chicken but not at hd3, N = 10^3. Whether to push that cell to a converged answer (cap 24,000+, hours) is your call; hd6
   at N = 10^3 needs a code fix before it can be re-solved.
3. **No THEORY claim is touched.** The headline chain results (lim_N PD, modal and K-arm cooperation, dollar traps, enforcement, union, three-player) stand; the audit's
   new content is a list of which published chain cells are converged under enlargement and which are not.
4. **A rule for the solvers.** A small unexplored flux ("cut") is not a convergence certificate: the cells that moved had cuts of 10^-15 or an unexplored flux of 0.999 at the
   cap. The honest flag is "candidates still above θ at the cap". Suggested default: print UNCONVERGED and refuse to report when that count is positive.
5. **What was not done.** 30 groups are unresolved (list above): most are low-risk (complete monomorphic space), but the realizable-language carrier chains
   (milestone 2 controls, identity, prudent openness: static tables missing and supports hand-restricted), the gene arm, the n = 8 priced / certificate / fringe cells and the
   n = 7 sparse PD cells were not reached, and the union / three-player / concessions cells cannot be pushed to a converged answer with this solver (one more decade of θ costs
   hours and the cut falls by a factor 2 to 4). The host was shared and loaded, which is why hour-scale jobs were stopped.
