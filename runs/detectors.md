## What ran

- **Gate:** GO (`runs/detectors-gate.json`): the Key clause is conservative (240/240 stored checks identical in value
  and step count), every accepted grudge's atom is true, forged grudges on non-readers and negated readers are rejected,
  host replay = term on all gate checks.
- **World K** (deviation 1): hypothesis-closed D certificates keyed to the partner they were produced against. K tables
  by the milestone-5 closure under the keyed reading (17,375 units), grudge pair tables (275 writer units, exact).
  Validation: world L with the grudge kernel reproduces stored scaling, Pangaea and annealed runs draw for draw; the
  two-bit reduction agrees with explicit-term host replay on 54/54 live pairs.
- **Part A:** 3,550 single-arrival assays (N = 200, 800: 5 arms × 3 predators × 100; N = 3,200: 3 arms × 50; second
  arrivals 400). **Part B:** 10 cells × 20 runs. **Part C:** 12 cells × 20 runs, except Cff_KGh_e0 with 4 (§2.7).
  **Reference:** 20 runs. **Post hoc:** mechanism decompositions (28 runs × 10 times), 201 matched replays.
- Compute: about 25 worker-hours, including about 4 h of horizontal-transmission runs killed unfinished (§2.7).

(B1: 50/50 founders; B2: natural shares, DET 3.4% of founders.)

- **Without grudges DET loses on islands before the merge** (0.50 → 0.23 by 200) and dies within 30–140 generations of it.
  Mechanism (post-hoc decomposition, reps 0–3): before the merge 10–27% of DET–PBN pairs are PBN exploiting DET (anti-gentle
  PBN members such as `if(CHK(them,^C,D),C,D)`, and FairBot-shaped members whose check of a DET member regresses), 30–40%
  feud (DET's rival sub-family); DET's fitness from PBN partners is −0.07 to −0.26 against PBN's −0.00 to −0.05. After
  the merge the surviving DET members feud with the PBN majority (0.75 of pairs) and lose as a minority.
- **With grudges the outcome reverses at equal mass:** grudges shut the exploitation down (PBN-exploits-DET pairs
  0.15 → 0.02 by generation 50; some DET members now defect on PBN members that once defected on them, 0.05–0.07 of
  pairs), DET is 0.62 at the merge, and from about generation 300 every DET–PBN pair is mutual and fitness differences
  are zero: PBN's removal (median generation ~3,000) is neutral drift of a minority inside one network.
- **The tail rates change the post-merge drift only** (pre-merge trajectories are identical across K = 0, 1, 3):
  prudence-premium removals 0 in every B cell. Sucker exposure (production encounters with a unit that cooperates with
  DefectBot-H) has medians 9,969 / 11,659 / 13,392 for K0 at K = 0 / 1 / 3 and 268,221 / 297,319 / 296,904 for KG; the
  tail adds about 33 / 94 arrivals per run (≈ 15 / 45 suckers), small against the network's own DefectBot-cooperating
  members.
- **Tail takeovers:** 1 (B1 K0, K = 1) and 3 + 3 (B2, K = 1) runs end all-defect: the merged PBN population drifts into
  members that cooperate with certified defectors (sucker share → 1.0), and a single tail defector takes all 12,800 in
  under 100 generations (labelled *unresolved*: the 50-generation checkpoints miss the ≥ 0.3 crossing).

## Removal causes (frozen labels, notes §1.5) and the matched replays

Headline labels over all network removals (counts in `runs/detectors/tables.md`; overlap in `runs/detectors.json`):

- Removals in the first 500 generations carry the **inefficiency** headline almost always, because their window starts
  in the seed's scramble (island P(C,C) < 0.95 by construction). This is the frozen definition; it does not identify a
  mechanism.
- **Predation** co-labels: B1 K0 20/20, B2 16–18/20, Cff 4–8/20 per cell. **Replays removing the exploiters of the
  window's modal resident never prevent the removal (0 of 84).**
- **Prudence premium:** 0 headline in B; in C as a co-label 1–4 per cell (and 1 headline, Cis K0 K = 1). Replays replacing
  every DefectBot-cooperating unit by DefectBot-H at the window's start prevent the DET removal in 0/10 Cff and 4/9 Cis
  cases.
- **The same sucker surgery prevents every B1 K0 DET removal it was applied to (15/15)** and 6/20 in B2: in B1 the
  DefectBot-cooperating units are PBN's anti-gentle members, the detectors' exploiters. So what removes DET at equal mass
  is direct exploitation by members of the PrudentBot network that punish gentleness, then minority coordination after
  the merge.
- **Abundance:** every PBN removal under KG (13 / 8 / 12 in B1) and the late DET removals: neutral drift inside a mutual
  network.
