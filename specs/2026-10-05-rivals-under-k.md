# Spec: rivals under the bounded prover K: does incompleteness remove the bridge-less obstruction?, 2026-10-05

Status: reviewed by gpt-6.1-sol (`reviews/2026-10-05-rivals-under-k-gpt-6.1-sol.md`) and revised; changes marked
[after review]. To be run by an Opus subagent. Joins RESULTS "Bridge-less rivals" (DEFERRED 1) and "K at n = 8" /
"K with the 4-rule" (DEFERRED 2). [after review] This is finite-cutoff (n = 8), finite-horizon evidence about the
bridge-less obstruction *relative to FairBot's pair*; it is not a large-population or large-cutoff conclusion, and
incompatible non-A networks, longer bridge paths and larger cutoffs stay untested here.

## Why

Under the free box about a quarter of the rival mass of FairBot's pair is literally bridge-less and permanent on the
tested scales, 0.98–0.99 of it the P\* family `and(BOX1(THEM(·)),not(BOX(THEM(·))))`, which cooperates iff
cooperation is provable from PA + Con but not from PA. In the bounded prover K the P\* family self-defects at every
*tested* budget (b ≤ 54, from audited derivations: the atoms it needs are structurally underivable up to 2L + 10,
"K with the 4-rule"; the mechanism is the missing distribution under a box, with bounded self-consistency the
separate obstruction for PB2) [after review: stated at the tested budgets, not "every budget"]. So under K at those
budgets these programs are not establishers and cannot be rivals. The question is whether anything takes their
place: if the bridge-less share collapses and natural separation falls accordingly, the bounded prover removes the
one obstruction to metapopulation universality found along I ≫ N at this cutoff; if K creates bridge-less rivals of
its own, the obstruction belongs to source reading as such.

## Definitions [after review]

- **Bridge-less (direct):** no pairwise bridge (a cooperative class mutually cooperating with FairBot,
  `BOX1(THEM(ME))` and R). Reported separately: **mediator path** (a path of length ≤ 3 in the establisher
  mutual-cooperation graph from R to A through cooperative classes), **zero transition** (one-migrant fixation of R
  into A and of A into R exactly 0 under the kernel), and **below threshold** (fixation < 10⁻¹² but positive).
- **Permanent:** 0 losses with the exposure stated and the one-sided 95% upper hazard bound 3/exposure; the
  limiting object is the hazard's N-scaling (exponential in N for coordination barriers), not the finite horizon.
- **New rival under K:** a source (canonical program), not an arm-specific class, that is a rival of A under K's
  table and not under the free table; identity is by source across arms.
- **Cooperative establishment** of a forced lineage: an island whose holder is that lineage *and* certified
  cooperative; **lineage survival:** the lineage present at the horizon in any state. Both reported.
- **Lumping validity:** each behavioural class under K has identical directed rows and columns for all its members
  (checked exhaustively), source masses and seed probabilities are preserved, and the establishment and bridge
  tests agree between the lumped and the source-level tables on a sample of 1,000 sources.

## Design

**Play table.** K at n = 8, global budget b = 16, guard at the reader's own budget (`runs/k-at-n8/kval_n8_b16.npy`
or regenerated by `src/k_at_n8.py`; sibling worktrees hold copies); the class table built as `src/seeds_in_n.py`
builds the free arm's, with the lumping validity check above; the free-arm pipeline must reproduce the existing
n = 8 free cache from the free table.

**Static screening** (free and K b = 16 at n = 8; K at b = 4 and 54 as budget sensitivity): establishers; rivals of
A (full and half); for each rival its direct bridges, mediator paths, prey, A-faked status, one-migrant fixations
at N = 200 (zero / below threshold / positive); the hard bridge-less mass and its share of rival mass under each
definition; the full establisher compatibility/bridge graph summarized (components, path-length distribution to A);
new rivals under K by source identity. Masses normalized and raw.

**Lottery** (kernel of `src/rival_islands.py` / `src/bridgeless_rivals.py` with K's class table; n = 8; N = 200,
I = 64; horizon 10⁵; holder rule; hazards with exposure; binomial intervals; runs as the independent units;
resolution times right-censored and reported) [after review: both natural arms first; calibrated and common mN]:
- (a) *Natural, matched:* 3,000 iid runs under K b = 16 and 3,000 under the free box at n = 8, each at **its own
  calibrated boundary mN** (T_nuc measured at m = 0 for each table, the dimensionless x = mN·T_nuc/N reported) **and
  at a common mN** (the free arm's boundary value), so calculus and migration are not confounded; every
  separation's rival source, bridge presence at seeding, bridge fate and resolution time recorded.
- (b) *Positive control:* the heaviest **bridged** rival of A under K, forced densely (one copy per island), 100
  runs: the mediator/holder machinery must resolve a known rivalry under K (mediation-before-loss reported).
- (c) *Forced P\* under K:* one P\* copy per island, founders **added** to the iid seed (the island's N kept by
  replacing one uniformly chosen seed, stated), 100 runs, with an **inert-defector control** (one D copy per
  island, same replacement rule) to separate founder-demography effects from P\*'s play; report cooperative
  establishment, lineage survival at the horizon, and q_est against the iid control.
- (d) *Forced bridge-less rival under K, if the screening finds one:* dense and sparse, 100 runs each.
- (e) *Continuation:* every unresolved separated run of (a) continued to 3·10⁵ generations.
Priority: screening → (a) both arms → (b) → (c) → (e) → (d). ≤ 3 workers; stop where time runs out and say so.
The K table at n = 9 is not attempted.

## RE predictions (with falsifiers) [after review]

1. **Under K at b = 16 the direct-bridge-less hard rival mass is < 0.02 of rival mass** (against ≈ 0.25 under the
   free box at n = 8), with no new rival of mass ≥ 10⁻⁶ by source identity. *Falsifier:* share ≥ 0.1, or a new
   bridge-less rival of mass ≥ 10⁻⁵. Grey zone 0.02–0.1: inconclusive.
2. **Natural horizon separation under K is lower than under the free box at the same cutoff and the same mN, and
   no horizon separation under K involves a direct-bridge-less rival.** The RE expects 0–2 of 3,000 under K
   (one-sided 95% upper rate ≈ 0.0021 at two events, which is a *bound*, not a fivefold reduction) against 4–20
   under free (mostly P\*-family). *Falsifier:* K's count ≥ free's count at the common mN, or a direct-bridge-less
   rival among K's horizon separations. Grey zone: K below free but with ≥ 3 events.
3. **Forced P\* under K has no cooperative establishment** (0 islands in 100 runs) **but may survive as a lineage**
   (the RE predicts lineage survival at the horizon in ≤ 0.1 of runs, no more than the inert-defector control's)
   and leaves q_est unchanged (|Δq_est| ≤ 0.1 against the iid control, and within 0.05 of the inert-defector
   control). *Falsifier:* a cooperative P\* establishment, or lineage survival exceeding the inert control's by
   ≥ 0.2. Grey zone between.
4. **Budget sensitivity:** the direct-bridge-less share under K is below 0.05 at b = 4, 16 and 54. *Falsifier:*
   ≥ 0.1 at some budget; grey zone 0.05–0.1.
5. **The positive control resolves** (mediation-before-loss ≥ 0.7 for the forced bridged rival under K) **and
   island-level efficiency is unchanged** (island P(C,C) ≥ 0.97 in every K cell; K's run-level efficient fraction
   not below the free box's by ≥ 0.05). *Falsifier:* mediation-before-loss ≤ 0.4, or K below free by ≥ 0.05.
