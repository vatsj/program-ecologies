## Why

Islands let a few clique copies win locally: per-copy establishment was about 0.08 at N = 100 and 0.04 at
N = 200–400, and at N ≥ 200 clique islands are permanent. In one well-mixed population a clique's growth is
proportional to its own frequency (≈ μ_Q ≈ 7·10⁻⁴ of the seed), while the open network's is proportional to the mass
of everything compatible with it; and a prudent cooperator does at least as well as a defector at every frequency
(both exploit unconditional cooperators; only the prudent one also cooperates with its own kind). So on a Pangaea
the open network should win deterministically and cliques should vanish. The interesting object is the continuum:
at fixed total population N·I, where along I does the clique start to win?

## Amendments after review (gpt-6.1-sol and gemini-4-argon; these supersede the text above where they conflict)

1. **Production held constant per program.** The primary schedule fixes production encounters per program per
   generation across the whole continuum at the (200, 64) baseline's value (2r = 200, i.e. r = 100 per Moran event),
   decoupling production from island size; the stored cells are rerun under it. The native r = N/2 runs beside it up to
   (800, 16), with production attempts, certificates produced and coverage through establishment reported for both,
   so a Pangaea win cannot be read as repaired production.
2. **One founder multiset per replicate.** For each of 20 seeds, one list of N·I founders is drawn and partitioned
   uniformly at random into the I islands of every geometry, so seeded copies of every family and every spelling are
   identical across the continuum; individual-identity multiplicities are recorded.
3. **Controls added.** A free-certificate Pangaea (full lists, as H) at (12,800, 1); deterministic frequency dynamics
   for the seed's families with certificates saturated, beside the actual runs; the C-donor arm distinguishes the
   donor's demographic extinction from failure of certificate spread.
4. **Fission–fusion cell.** (100, 128) for 200 generations at calibrated mN, then all islands merged into one
   population of 12,800 (and a variant merging at 1,000).
5. **Endpoints.** On a Pangaea: (a) P(C,C) ≥ 0.95; (b) the dominant compatible network holds ≥ 0.99 of certified
   carriers; and (c) its raw population share, with neutral remnants (unconditional cooperators, uncertified
   carriers) reported separately. Establishment and absorption are defined independently of the reporting horizon;
   unresolved trajectories are reported apart from losses. Early extinctions of prudent founders, bridges and individual
   clique identities are logged.
6. **Uncertainty.** Exact binomial intervals; thresholds are predictive point thresholds with predeclared pass / fail /
   inconclusive regions (20/20 has a one-sided 95% lower bound near 0.86); conclusions are restricted to the sizes run.
