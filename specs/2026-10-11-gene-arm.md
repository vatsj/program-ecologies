# Spec: the gene arm: parametric base strategies, special-case genes, a credibility filter, and spontaneous generation, 2026-10-11

Status: draft by the RE (Fable) from the RS's proposal (DEFERRED 12, 2026-10-11); to be reviewed by gpt-6.1-sol; to be
run by an Opus subagent.

## The RS's framing (verbatim, registered)

> I think the interpretation of these certificates as "genes" ... helps make sense of commitment races as a sort
> of co-evolutionary arms race in a competitive game. Some threats are fit ... I'd like to confer the union the power
> to special-case when it sees a quorum, but don't love the idea of letting the bosses special-case a threat.
> ... I propose that certain classes of credible deviation get encoded as a gene on the spot. This suggests an
> interpretation of a gene as a deviation from an equilibrium condition on the underlying program. I'm excited about
> this as a proof device: no credible deviations (say, pareto improvements) exist, bc if they did they would've been
> adopted as a fit gene. I propose we stress-test this for existing equilibrium notions ... mb bayes-nash? The
> strategies take some closed parametric form (... a single-digit number of parameters. The point is we can't j
> hard-code genes) and genes are flexible enough to express anything strategies miss.

## Why

The RS's asymmetry ("the union may special-case on a quorum; the boss may not special-case a threat") has a measured
counterpart: the RC arm of RESULTS "Incentive-compatible enforcement" (boss enforcement ex-post rational, workers'
quorum strike committed) gives the fair wage 0.55 under the uniform prior, the best arm. But RC declares the
asymmetry. The principled version is a **credibility filter on genes**: a gene is admissible iff executing its
override is ex-post rational for its carrier at the point it fires. A whack costs c when executed and deters only
if never executed, so it fails the filter; a quorum strike at zero wage costs nothing, so it passes. The run asks
whether selection among filtered genes *recovers* RC's asymmetry without declaring it, what spontaneous generation
(genes proposed as credible deviations) does as a mutation kernel, and whether "no credible deviation exists at the
stable state" is a usable equilibrium notion against Nash and Bayes–Nash with roles as public types.

## Design

**Games.** (i) The committed social organization game (CC base; RESULTS "The union game": boss wage s ∈ {0, ¼, ½},
whack h ∈ {none, 1, 2} at c = 0.5, L = 1; two workers work/strike; QUORUM available to workers) in the modal arm
(`src/union_enforcement*.py`, `src/concessions.py`); (ii) fixed-role chicken (`src/fixed_role_chicken.py`, the
`chicken` table).

**Base strategies: closed parametric form, single-digit parameters.** Workers: (t, q) with t ∈ {0, ¼, ½, ∞} the
wage threshold below which the worker strikes and q ∈ {0, 1} strike iff the other worker is certified to strike
(quorum); 8 base workers [after review: retaliation dropped, since a "next encounter" trait cannot enter a memoryless
payoff table]. The threshold–quorum interaction is fixed: strike iff (wage < t) or (q and the quorum atom holds).
Bosses: (s, h, p) with s the wage, h the whack policy, p ∈ {0, 1, 2} the probe level (0 none; 1 pay ½ iff w1 certified
to strike at 0; 2 the full price discriminator), probes read as in RESULTS "Concessions" (counterfactual play against
a quoted constant boss); 27 base bosses. Chicken: (a, b) with a ∈ {Straight, Swerve} the default and b ∈ {0, 1}
"swerve iff the opponent is certified Straight"; 4 per role. [after review] **Matched base-commitment controls:**
because base bosses retain committed whacks and base chicken strategies retain Straight, every filtered arm is also
run on bases with the commitment removed (bosses with h = none only; chicken bases without the firm default), so that
"the filter recovers RC" is tested against bases that cannot smuggle a non-credible commitment past it.

**Genes.** A gene is (trigger, override): the trigger a modal atom over the encounter (QUORUM, a probe, a box about
the opponent's play, a whack having occurred), the override an action at the special-cased point (strike/work;
wage/whack; Straight/Swerve). A program is a base strategy plus a list of ≤ 2 genes, evaluated by the modal
evaluator with the gene's override replacing the base action where its trigger holds. [after review] **Operational
semantics, frozen:** when two genes fire, the first listed wins; a mutation that adds a gene at the cap replaces the
last listed; a gene's trigger is evaluated on the encounter's own world chain as the base atoms are; the
special-cased point is declared in the program text. The gene grammar is enumerated; the **gene prior** is the
length prior over (base, genes) with each gene costing its node count. [after review] **Encoding invariance is a
prerequisite, measured:** for every named gene the behaviourally equivalent base encoding (where one exists in the
base grammar) is listed, and the filter's verdict and the chain's π must not depend on the packaging; where they
do, the report says so and the equilibrium notion below is stated as representation-relative.

**Two best replies** [after review]: the *continuation* best reply (others' actions at the point fixed, the history
held) and the *mutant-program* best reply (the whole encounter re-evaluated with the carrier's readable program
replaced, since replacing a readable program changes others' certificates, probes and actions). The credibility
filter uses the continuation notion (the RS's "credible when it fires"); gene-stability and every evolutionary
deviation use the mutant-program notion.

**Arms.** (G-all) every gene admissible; (G-cred) **the override filter**: a gene is admissible iff, at every
encounter of the **frozen reference catalogue** (all unfiltered programs; admitted-only and residents-only are run
as sensitivity checks, since filtering against survivors is circular and order-dependent) where its trigger fires,
the override is a continuation best reply for its carrier; the report lists each gene's firing witnesses and marks
**vacuous admissions** (triggers that never fire in the reference catalogue); (G-cred-whole) [after review]
**whole-program credibility**: the same test applied to every behaviour, base and gene alike, at every point (the
per-point execution rule of the RR arm applied to the whole program), which is the arm that can recover RC's
*execution* asymmetry rather than its packaging; (G-asym) the RS's declared asymmetry (workers may carry quorum
genes; bosses may carry no whack gene); the reference arms CC, RC, RR of the enforcement run on the same catalogue;
and [after review] a **random-removal filter** control (the same number of genes removed at random, original prior
masses preserved and the renormalization reported). **Spontaneous generation** (SG): a mutation kernel that, at a
resident state, proposes with probability ½ a gene whose trigger is a condition true at that state and whose
override is the carrier's mutant-program best reply there (a credible deviation), and otherwise draws from the
gene prior; run as (G-cred, SG) and (G-all, SG) beside the prior-only kernels and [after review] beside a
**uniform admissible-local-mutation kernel** (a uniformly random admissible gene added, no targeting), so that SG's
targeting is separated from its mutation support. SG is a mutation kernel, charged like any mutant; fitness is the
chain's, never assumed (discipline rule 3). [after review] **Coalitional deviations** (two workers' joint quorum
strike) are not reachable by a unilateral rare-mutation step; where the RS's proof device needs them, a
**coalition kernel** (two simultaneous mutations in two slots at rate ε²) is run as a labelled separate cell, and
sequential paths through a neutral intermediate are enumerated.

**Static.** Catalogues per arm (classes after lumping, where [after review] lumping is established for the payoff
kernel *and* the mutation kernel, since SG depends on syntactic gene accessibility; syntactic twins are retained
where it is not, and the audited log-domain closure-seeded solver is used throughout); which genes each filter
admits and removes (the whack gene, the quorum gene, the probe-concession gene, the chicken "firm" gene), with
firing witnesses and vacuous admissions; **gene-stable states**: states at which no admissible single gene is a
strict mutant-program improvement for any carrier, under a stated **gene-completeness** (which deviations the ≤ 2-gene
grammar can express, listed), and their relation to Nash of the role game: states Nash but not gene-stable,
gene-stable but not Nash, and both, with the actual Pareto frontier of each game reported so that "Pareto
improvement" is read off; [after review] roles as public types add no Bayesian uncertainty, so Bayes–Nash is noted
as coinciding with Nash here and not scored.

**Chain** (ε→0; N = 10³, 10⁴; the uniform prior over named programs and the length prior, as in the enforcement
run): π over wage / outcome classes, support, transitions, the decisive edges' Δ and N·Δ, for each arm and kernel;
the fair share, the ¼ share, zero wage, the whack share; in chicken the one-sided norms and the bridge exits.

**Lottery** (ε = 0, the social organization lottery's conventions, (100, 64), mN = 1, 40 runs per arm): fair
establishment and persistence, the whacker's fate, under G-all, G-cred, G-asym with the prior kernel; and with SG
as the seeding kernel's innovation step applied at each generation with rate 1/N (reported separately as a
different object, finite-ε innovation, never pooled with the ε = 0 cells).

**Scale guard.** The modal evaluator at n = 6 with ≤ 2 genes; report class counts per arm before any chain; if an arm
exceeds 3,000 classes, cap genes at 1 and report it as a different experiment. ≤ 3 workers; kill pool children by pid.

## RE predictions (with falsifiers)

[after review: success and falsifier ranges meet; an outcome in neither is a miss of the number.]

1. **The override filter alone does not recover RC; whole-program credibility does** [revised after review]: G-cred
   removes the whack gene but base bosses keep their committed whacks, so G-cred's fair under the uniform prior
   stays within 0.1 of CC's 0.31; G-cred on the base-commitment control and G-cred-whole each put fair within 0.1 of
   RC's 0.55. *Falsifier:* G-cred ≥ 0.45 (packaging alone recovers RC), or G-cred-whole ≤ 0.4.
2. **G-all is CC with more fakers:** under G-all the whack gene deters as in CC and fair ≤ CC's; under the length
   prior every gene arm sits at zero wage ≥ 0.4. *Falsifier:* G-all fair > RC's.
3. **Targeted SG favours concessions and accommodation, not whacks** [revised after review]: a costly executed whack
   is never a best reply, so SG's targeted half proposes quorum strikes and concession genes; (G-cred, SG) fair is
   ≥ (G-cred, prior) at N = 10⁴ under the length prior, with the uniform admissible-local kernel in between; no
   fairness increase is guaranteed under G-all. *Falsifier:* SG lowering G-cred's fair by ≥ 0.1, or the targeted
   half proposing a whack gene as a best reply.
4. **Gene-stability and Nash are incomparable unless gene-completeness is total** [revised after review]: with the
   ≤ 2-gene grammar some gene-stable states are not Nash of the role game (a deviation the grammar cannot express)
   and some Nash states are not gene-stable (zero wage with a non-credible threat, under G-cred-whole); the fair
   state is gene-stable under G-cred-whole and gene-unstable under G-all by an actual source-re-evaluated witness.
   *Falsifier:* every gene-stable state Nash and every Nash state gene-stable (coincidence), or the fair state
   gene-unstable under G-cred-whole.
5. **The RS's proof device holds one gene deep and needs a coalition kernel beyond** [revised after review]: at the
   chain's top states under G-cred-whole no admissible single gene is a strict mutant-program improvement for its
   carrier; the exit from zero wage is not a single mutation but a sequential path through a neutral intermediate
   (a quorum gene entering neutrally at zero wage, then the second worker's strict switch), and the coalition kernel
   at ε² reaches the same destination faster. *Falsifier:* a one-gene strict improvement at a top state, or no
   sequential path and no coalition path from zero wage.
6. **Chicken: the firm gene fails the filter and the norm survives anyway,** since constants carry it; on the
   base-commitment control the norm forms only through readers; G-cred's chain and lottery match the constants arm of
   RESULTS "Fixed-role chicken" within 0.1. *Falsifier:* the firm gene admitted, or a difference ≥ 0.25.
7. **Encoding invariance fails somewhere** [added after review]: at least one named gene has a base encoding on
   which the override filter's verdict differs (the filter sees the gene's override point and not the base's), so the
   equilibrium notion is representation-relative unless whole-program credibility is used. *Falsifier:* every named
   gene's verdict invariant under re-encoding in every arm.

The RS is invited to add predictions; this run is his proposal, and the uncertain ones are 3 and 5.
