# Spec: K with the 4-rule, and a classifier for what a sound bounded prover loses, 2026-10-05

Status: reviewed by gpt-6.1-sol (`reviews/2026-10-05-k-four-gpt-6.1-sol.md`) and revised; changes marked [after
review]. To be run by an Opus subagent. Follow-up to RESULTS "K at n = 8" (DEFERRED 2, "would settle it next").
Cheap: one evaluator change with its soundness proof, the n = 6 and n = 8 tables and chains, and a frozen
classifier evaluated on the whole catalogue.

## Why

K, the sound bounded calculus, keeps and improves the n = 8 free arm, and the whole gain is the removal of 37 faker
classes, mostly Gödel sentences, which K cannot prove things about. K's incompleteness is selective: it loses the
cooperation of P\*, P2, PB2 = `and(BOX(THEM(ME)),BOXD2(THEM(^D)))` and the level-2 ladder, and keeps PrudentBot. Two
things follow:

1. **A 4-rule for K.** Box truth in K is the syntactic existence of a derivation, so from a derivation of ⊢ A of size
   s ≤ a, Nec yields a derivation of ⊢ □_a A of size s + 1, and □_{a+1} □_a A is true. A rule Γ, □_a A ⊢ □_{a+1} □_a A, Δ
   as an initial-sequent form is what PB2 and the ladder need under a Löb hypothesis (the K-at-n8 diagnosis: "□□⊥ from
   □P[D, PB2]"). [after review] The soundness of K+4 is **to be proved**, not asserted: state the proof encoding and
   the cost recurrence of every rule (including indexed Nec, BoxEq, JLöb and the new rule), then prove by induction
   on derivation size that every K+4-derivable sequent is true in the standard model, with the 4-rule's case
   constructed from the Nec witness and its certified bound; "0 violations" from the checker is a regression test,
   not the proof. The question is then: **do the Con/4-axiom cooperators return, and do the Gödel-sentence fakers
   return with them?**
2. **The faker-removal control of "K at n = 8" was identified from K's own table.** [after review] A classifier
   computed from the free arm and GL alone can make it independent only if it is *frozen before evaluation* and
   evaluated on the *whole eligible catalogue*, not on the 37 removed classes; and a trace-level classifier is an
   empirical instrument, not a characterization theorem, since one minimal GL derivation does not show that every
   derivation uses the designated premises, nor does failure to translate it show K-underivability.

## Definitions [after review]

- **Con statement:** a subformula ¬□^k⊥ (k ≥ 1) occurring under a box in the derivation. **Gödel-sentence premise:**
  a boxed formula □B used as a Löb-rule hypothesis where B is not GL-provable at level 0 (decided by the GL
  decision procedure of `src/gl_proofs.py`, independent of any bounded search) and ¬B is the program's own play
  constant or its unfolding.
- **Structural incompleteness** of K for a GL-true atom: not K-derivable at any budget ≤ 2·(its GL length) + 10.
  **Finite-budget failure:** K-derivable at some budget above the tested one. Reported separately.
- **Eligible catalogue:** every (invader, resident) pair at n = 8 in which the invader strictly invades a supported
  self-cooperator in the free arm's chain at N = 10⁴ (the resident set and the invasion criterion of "K at n = 8").
- **Source-level masses are retained across arms:** behavioural classes are recomputed per arm, but every chain uses
  the source-level mutation weights, and splits/merges of classes between arms are tracked with their aggregate
  weights, so no arm changes the prior.

## Design

**Part A: K+4** (`src/bounded_k.py`, the rule as an option, default off; the K tables of "K at n = 8" must
reproduce exactly with it off, in its own commit). Deliver first `notes/k-four.md` §1: the encoding, cost recurrence
and the inductive soundness proof of K+4. Then: the checker (0 violations required) on the n = 6 and n = 8 tables;
K+4 tables at n = 6 (b = 4, 16, 40) and n = 8 (b = 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 54) with **dense sweeps around each
re-arming threshold** (every b from 4 to 40 for PB2, P\*, P2, the ladder members and the 21 Gödel sentences, with the
certified witness cost at the threshold) [after review]; tests on nested boxes, cross-budget boundaries and
generated formulas outside the catalogue; the leak test at fixed b and on the cross-budget catalogue; the chain at
n = 8 for K+4 at b = 16 and 54 against K and the free arm (P(C,C) at N = 10³, 10⁴, 3·10⁴; support; transitions; the
dominant entry and exit rates with their N-scaling, reported separately from the finite-N numbers; the K+4 faker set
defined as for K, with the classes whose cooperation K+4 restores listed separately from those whose exploitation it
restores); the lottery at (100, 64), mN = 1, 20 paired seeds, b = 16.

**Part B: the frozen classifier** [after review: an instrument, not a theorem]. Before any K+4 table is computed,
commit the classifier: for an eligible pair, take the minimal GLS+Def derivation of the invader's play against the
resident (from `src/gl_proofs.py`) and flag it if some Löb-rule application has a Gödel-sentence premise or a Con
statement under a box. Evaluate it on the **entire eligible catalogue** (both labels: K-disarmed at b = 16 or not),
reporting the full confusion matrix, false positives included, and the same against the K+4 set; then on the n = 6
catalogue as a held-out cutoff. For every misclassified pair, report whether K's failure is structural or
finite-budget (definitions above) and whether an alternative GL derivation without the trigger exists (search for
one up to twice the minimal size). State what the classifier establishes (an enrichment, with its precision and
recall) and what it does not.

**Part C (required) [after review]:** matched table interventions in the free chain at n = 8: (i) the free table
with only the classifier-flagged classes deleted; (ii) with only the K+4-restored cooperators' plays taken from K+4
(exploitation unchanged); (iii) with only the K+4-restored exploiting plays taken (cooperation unchanged); each
against K, K+4 and the free arm at N = 10⁴, to separate restored cooperation from restored exploitation and the
redistribution of invasion targets.

Priority: A (notes §1, option, reproduction) → B (freeze, evaluate on K's set) → A (tables, sweeps, chain) → B
(K+4 set, held-out) → C → lottery. ≤ 3 workers.
