# Spec: K with analytic cut (distribution under a box): does P\* return, and do its fakers return with it?, 2026-10-05

Status: reviewed by gpt-6.1-sol (`reviews/2026-10-05-k-cut-gpt-6.1-sol.md`) and revised; changes marked [after
review]. To be run by an Opus subagent. Follow-up to RESULTS "K with the 4-rule" (DEFERRED 2, "would settle it

## Why

K's incompleteness is selective and, as of "K with the 4-rule", fully explained: PB2 needs its own bounded
consistency (bounded Gödel II; recovered by the guard convention), while P\*, P2, the level-2 ladder and the
Con-sentence fakers (`not(BOX(THEM(^C)))` and the readers that quote it) all need **distribution under a box**
(□A, □(A → B) ⊢ □B inside a Löb branch), which K has in no form. The trade-off the program cares about is whether
*completeness buys fakers*: the free oracle has P\* (0.053 of π at N = 3·10⁴) and its Gödel-sentence fakers, and K
has neither, which is why K cooperates more. A sound bounded prover with distribution is the direct test. [after
review] The primary signal is *logical restoration* (which atoms become derivable, for cooperators and for fakers),
not the aggregate P(C,C), which can hide restored fakers of negligible mass or without accessible victims.

## The calculus K_c [after review: defined before implementation]

- **Cut rule with contexts:** from Γ ⊢ C, Δ and Γ, C ⊢ Δ infer Γ ⊢ Δ; size = 1 + size(left) + size(right). The
  **cut formula C** ranges over the **reachable set** R: the finite set of formulas that are subformulas of the
  unfolded definitions φ_x[y] of the (finite) catalogue's programs, their box contents, and the guards, closed under
  one unfolding of a definitional constant. R is finite because the catalogue, the definitional constants and the
  DSL syntax are finite and unfolding is applied once per constant occurrence (no recursive re-unfolding in R).
- **Search invariant:** every premise of every rule has size strictly less than its conclusion, so iterative
  deepening on size terminates and "within b" is decidable; cycles cannot arise because a sequent's derivation never
  contains itself (sizes strictly decrease). The GL-erasure prune stays valid because cut is GL-admissible, so
  erasing a K_c derivation still yields a GL+Def theorem; verify the prune on derivations *containing* cut, not only
  on their conclusions.
- **Distribution under a box as a derived rule:** exhibit, before any table, the minimal K_c derivation of
  □_a A, □_c (A → B) ⊢ □_d B with explicit indices and contexts (the expected route: JLöb or Nec on a cut between the
  two witnesses, giving d = a + c + 2 or a stated bound), and prove **witness composition**: if ⊢ A has a derivation
  of size ≤ a and ⊢ A → B one of size ≤ c, then ⊢ B has one of size ≤ a + c + 1 (the cut itself), so □_{a+c+1} B is
  true; and the **Löb/cut interaction**: a cut inside a JLöb premise (context □_b S) is still a closed derivation
  of that premise, so the JLöb witness construction of `notes/proof-length.md` §3 is unchanged. The soundness
  theorem of K_c is then the K proof with one more case (cut: both premises true ⇒ conclusion true). Hand-checked
  positive and negative distribution examples, witness replay by the independent checker, and a test that every K
  derivation remains a K_c derivation.

## Design

1. **Soundness and decidability first** (`notes/k-cut.md` §1, as above), then the option in `src/bounded_k.py` (its
   own commit; K tables reproduce exactly with it off).
2. **Tables** at n = 6 (b = 4, 16, 40) and n = 8 (b = 4, 8, 16, 24, 54) with the soundness checker (0 violations
   required as a regression); dense sweeps over b = 4…54 for the named family (P\*, P2, P12b, P\*1b, PB2, PrudentBot,
   FairBot's pair, the 21 Gödel sentences and the 22 Con sentences of "K with the 4-rule"), with certified witness
   costs at each b\*; for every named atom not derivable at b = 54, a capped search up to b = 200 to separate
   **structural failure** from **cost above the tested budgets** [after review]; the K_c-provable fraction of
   GL-true atoms by count and μ-weight; the leak test at fixed b and on the cross-budget catalogue {4, 16};
   source-level masses retained and class splits/merges tracked.
3. **Chains** [after review: audited solver] at n = 8 (PD, w = 0.3) with the seeded log-domain solver and independent
   state discovery, twin drift, residuals and rate-level validation (`src/chain_log.py` from the solver audit if
   merged, else `src/modal_dollar.py`'s solver generalized to the PD), for K_c at b = 16 and 54 against K and the
   free arm: P(C,C) at N = 10³, 10⁴, 3·10⁴; support; transitions; dominant entry and exit rates with N-scaling.
   **Invasion hazards** reported both as source counts and μ-weighted, against a fixed victim panel (FairBot,
   `BOX1(THEM(ME))`, PrudentBot, P\*, `BOX(THEM(THEM))`) and against the endogenous supported victims.
4. **The 2×2 restoration design** [after review]: freeze on the full source catalogue the set R_coop of classes
   self-cooperating in K_c but not K, and R_expl of classes strictly invading a fixed-panel victim in K_c but not K
   (collision rule: a class in both sets is assigned to both, and the overlap is reported); build four tables from
   K's table: neither / R_coop's rows and columns replaced by K_c's / R_expl's (invader, victim) and (victim,
   invader) entries replaced by K_c's / both; solve all four at N = 10⁴ and report the two main effects and the
   interaction term. Hybrid tables are checked for consistency (every entry comes from exactly one source table).
5. **The guard convention crossed in** [after review]: K/K_c × own-budget/one-up guard at b = 16, N = 10⁴, four
   cells, with the interaction.
6. **Lottery** at (100, 64), mN = 1, 20 paired seeds, K_c at b = 16; success = every island held by a cooperator at
   a verified closed class; censored otherwise; Wilson intervals.

Priority 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6; ≤ 3 workers; stop where time runs out and say where.
