# Review of `specs/2026-10-24-exploration-audit.md` by gemini-4-argon

1. **Errors, contradictions or unsound mechanisms**
- *Multi-role monomorphic state-space formula:* Design §1 defines "the size of the complete monomorphic state space (K for one population, K₁ × K₂ for two roles)." Several published chains are three-role games (e.g., the social organization / union game with Boss, Union, and Replacement Pool or two worker slots; three-player divide-the-dollar; auctions with seller + bidders). **Fix:** Change to $\prod_{r} K_r$ across all roles $r$ (and $K$ for single-population $k$-player games).
- *Confounding exploration caps with twin-expansion in Stage 3:* Stage 3 claims to run "so that each change is attributable," ending with "(e) the twin-expanded distribution (IMPLEMENTATION §4)." However, if a published cell already used the twin-expanded distribution (which became standard after the 2026-10-05 solver audit), running (c) and (d) *without* twin expansion changes the transition model back to the lumped chain, confounding the stagewise attribution. **Fix:** Keep the transition model (lumped vs. twin-expanded) matched to the published cell through stages (a)–(d); apply (e) only to pre-2026-10-05 cells that were published under the lumped chain.
- *Unbounded runtime in Stage 3(d):* Stage 3(d) requires "a multi-start replicator search (supports up to 6), each retained state tested against every admissible invader." On large grammars ($K > 10^3$), testing all $\binom{K}{2}$ to $\binom{K}{6}$ seeds or running multi-start replicator dynamics from every monomorphic exit is computationally intractable and will trip the 2-hour stall/budget guard. **Fix:** Restrict the polymorphic search in (d) to transitions out of the recurrent monomorphic classes (or top-$\pi$ states covering 99.9% of mass) rather than the entire state space.

2. **Design flaws or confounds**
- *Simultaneous relaxation in Stage 3(b):* "every active restriction enlarged together (cap 10×, threshold lowered by 10⁴, invader top-k widened to all invaders...)." If a cell moves in 3(b) and is too large for 3(c) (as two-role and three-role games with $K_1 \times K_2 > 10^5$ will be), you will not know whether the failure was caused by `max_states` truncation, $\theta$-inflow pruning, or the `top-k` invader filter. Knowing which mechanism failed matters for future solver defaults. **Fix:** On any cell that moves by $>0.02$ in 3(b) and cannot reach 3(c), toggle the active restrictions individually (e.g., `top-k` widened alone vs. `max_states`/`θ` enlarged alone) to isolate the culprit.

3. **Predictions you think are wrong**
- *Prediction 3* names "the realizable-language chains (milestones 2 and 5, provenance, world K)" as the primary at-risk cells. By CLAUDE.md, milestone 2's chain is absorbed by a 6-node `CliqueBot` ("absorbs the ε→0 chain at every N and prior"), which is a strict equilibrium with zero neutral exits, and provenance/world K results are primarily $\varepsilon = 0$ seed-lottery runs ("the full chain not run"). These will either not be in the chain inventory or will not move when enlarged. **My prediction:** The >0.05 shifts will occur in multi-role asymmetric games (Incentive-compatible enforcement, Concessions, Gene arm, Coalition-proof auctions) where $\prod_r K_r$ is large and `max_states` / `top-k` caps truncated the accommodator/concession neutral networks, and in 2026-10-23 RPS.

4. **Missing controls or cheap additions**
- *Boundary-leakage diagnostic for cells stopping at Stage 3(b):* For cells too large for 3(c), "stable under this enlargement" is weak if the 10× cap is still binding on the recurrent set. **Fix:** Record the *frontier outflow mass* (stationary $\pi$-mass of explored states that still have unexpanded outgoing transitions or hit the cap) at both (a) and (b). If frontier outflow is $O(1)$, mark the cell *unconverged* even if (a) and (b) agree.
