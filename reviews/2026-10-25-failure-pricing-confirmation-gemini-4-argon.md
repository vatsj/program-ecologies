**1. Design flaws or confounds**
- **Missing fresh runs for RPS/inspection at $P_{\text{fail}} \in \{0, 1, 10\}$ under (100, 64).** Prediction 4 tests the new "RPS by mixing" score at 1 and 10, and the PD arm runs 0, 1, and 10 on fresh indices, but the RPS/inspection design block only specifies fresh runs at $\{0.03, 0.1, 0.3\}$ for (100, 64). Either specify that 1, 10, and 0 are scored on the stored exploratory rows from "What compute should cost" (mixing exploratory and confirmatory samples in the same table), or add 40 fresh runs for RPS at 0, 1, and 10 (cheap in $L_T$ at 2,000 generations).
- **Ambiguity in "end-state charge per play" for the PD.** In the lottery with S-nonself endogenous production, certificate production and failed reads happen during the founding scramble or upon migration, while a converged universal-open island at generation 10,000 plays cached/checked certificates with zero timeouts. Clarify whether "end-state charge per play" is the encounter-weighted average across the final generation's population, or the cumulative run-average charge. Also state explicitly the numerical value of the "PD payoff range" (e.g., $T - S$ vs $T - P$) in `notes/failure-pricing-confirm.md` before coding.
- **Statistical power vs. Prediction 1's falsifier.** At $n = 40$ runs, a true universal-open rate of $0.85$ (34/40) has a 95% Wilson/Clopper-Pearson lower bound of $\approx 0.708$ (barely "met"), while $33/40$ ($0.825$) has a lower bound of $\approx 0.675$ ("undetermined", since $0.70$ is inside the interval). This makes "met" vs "undetermined" noisy at $n = 40$. The bracketed falsifier for Prediction 1 (`PD undetermined with a point estimate below 0.75`, i.e., $< 30/40$) is well-calibrated, so ensure the prose of Prediction 1 matches its bracketed falsifier: count Prediction 1 as passed if PD is met *or* undetermined with point estimate $\ge 0.75$.

**2. Predictions I think are wrong**
- **Prediction 3 (RPS met by mixing at $P_{\text{fail}} = 0.3$).** In RPS, a timed-out read against a mutual reader or mixer incurs $0.3$ raw payoff penalty—comparable to the $0$ vs $\pm 1$ game stakes. At $P_{\text{fail}} = 1.0$, the previous run already shifted from mixing (`PRR`) to mutual non-play / constant fallback. There is a substantial chance that $0.3$ is already above the threshold where `PRR`'s guarded read is too costly during the scramble against other readers, dropping `PRR` island share and failing the "by-mixing" score (forfeits $> 0.05$ or constants taking over). *My prediction:* RPS by-mixing passes at $0.1$, is borderline or fails at $0.3$.
