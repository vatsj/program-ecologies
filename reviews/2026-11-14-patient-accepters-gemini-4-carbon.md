- **Total reader mass confound across arms:** F has reader mass $\delta$, while F+R has $(1+\gamma)\delta$. If F+R beats F, you cannot tell whether R's conditional acceptance caused it or simply doubling non-constant reader mass at the expense of constants. Fix: add a matched-mass row where F+R splits $\delta$ (e.g., $\delta/2$ F, $\delta/2$ R) at $N=200, \beta=3$, alongside the additive $\gamma\delta$ design.
- **Unpriced compute on R vs C⅓:** Under default $A_f = 0.1$ (failure/forfeit pricing), R pays *zero* compute cost when reading non-timing-out programs (constants, non-looping readers), contradicting the RS's premise that "compute and prior mass select out a program that reads." Fix: ensure Step 0 explicitly separates islands where the excluded slot times out R (incurring $P_{\text{fail}} = 0.1$, making $\rho \cdot N \ll 1$) from constant/clean-reader third slots where R is strictly neutral.

**2. Predictions I think are wrong**
- **RE 3 & RE 4 (F+R escaping unequal islands and restoring $\delta^* \le 0.1$ at $\beta=3$):** Likely wrong. For R to exit a bully-held island, R must first drift neutrally into the victim slot (prob $\sim 1/N$, slow, and blocked whenever the excluded slot holds a reader that causes a mutual-read timeout penalty of $0.1$). While R waits or enters, adding $\gamma\delta$ of ⅓-yielding programs to the founding scramble and migration pool *feeds Trojan bullies* ($\beta=3\delta$), accelerating unequal island formation in the first $30N$ (where Queue 76 showed outcomes settle). Moreover, once R pairs fairly at ½ with a ½-constant, R *has no guard against its old partner*: a ⅔-demander in R's default partner slot is ignored while the ½-offer holds, *unless* the ½-constant drifts out, instantly snapping back to unequal. Worse, on a fair island held by (R, ½-constant), a bully or exploiter can invade the ½-constant's slot if R's fallback subsidizes it. Prediction: F+R fails to reach $\delta^* \le 0.1$ at $\beta=3$ (V* stays $< 0.5$); in seeded all-unequal starts, F+R fair share at $120N$ will be $< 0.3$ because neutral victim turnover + neutral excluded-slot arrival takes longer than $120N$ at $mN=1, \Lambda=100$ against bully re-invasion.
- **RE 3 (F+O+R $\ge$ F+R):** If Opener O reads the victim slot to check whether to offer ½, and R reads the excluded slot (O), **R and O mutually read each other and time out!** Under `n` timeout convention and $A_f = 0.1$, both pay $-0.1$ and R does *not* switch to ½. Thus O cannot trigger R—only unguarded ½-constants can! Prediction: F+O+R performs *worse* than or equal to F+R (and Step 0.2 for O into R-victim will fail/timeout unless O is an unconditional ½-offerer, which contradicts O being a reader). Check O's exact AST before committing RE 3!
