# Review of `specs/2026-10-21-core-stage2.md` by gemini-4-argon

1. **Errors, contradictions or unsound mechanisms**
- *Ill-posed reverse resolution order:* "items first (they read P's source or certificate, never P's output), then `TX` (it reads the items' outputs)... As a check, every triple is also resolved in the reverse order, by independent fresh evaluation (no cached outputs), and the two must agree."
  In the reverse order (`TX` first, items second), `TX` is evaluated before the items have outputs, yet `TX`'s policy is a function of the winners' output total $T$. For `TX` to evaluate first without cached outputs, it must call the item evaluators to obtain their outputs $b_A, b_B$. Because followers only inspect `TX`'s syntax/certificate and never invoke `TX`'s evaluator, this call terminates without cycling. **Fix:** State explicitly that when `TX` evaluates first, querying an item's output triggers fresh evaluation of that item; order-independence holds because the call graph is a DAG, not because the two roles can be evaluated without calling each other.
- *Unbounded `cap` in the unrestricted space:* Constants are in `0..cap`, `TX(t)` in `1..cap`, and "Bluffing, in the unrestricted space only: `TX(t)` with t above P's value." Neither the brief nor `notes/core-rediscovery.md` states the numerical value of `cap` in the unrestricted space. **Fix:** State the exact integer `cap` for each instance in the unrestricted space (e.g., $\text{cap} = w(N) = 30, 28, 22$, or $2v_P$).

2. **Design flaws or confounds**
- *Confound in sucker-depleted rain (Control iv):* "[redefined after review]: item-role rain draws only followers (no constants), while P-role rain is unchanged."
  This changes two things simultaneously: it removes constant underbidders, and it multiplies the arrival rate of followers by $1/m$ (increasing both follower replenishment and $\delta$-diversity). Worse, once followers fix on an island, a rain of random $\delta \in \{0.1, \dots, 0.9\}$ includes low-$\delta$ followers; whenever an incoming $\delta_A$ pairs with the resident $\delta_B$ such that $b_A + b_B < t$, `TX(t)` wins and exploits them. Thus, follower-only rain *still supplies suckers* via $\delta$-mismatches!
  **Fix:** Add a control **(iv″)** where item-role rain draws only *safe constants* (constants $c \ge v_P/2$, or $c$ matching the resident follower's bid) or only followers with $\delta \ge 0.5$, while keeping follower rain mass at $m$. Log the encounter-level `TX` win rate ("tested" rate) in (iv) to check whether $\delta$-mismatch suckers are doing the work of constant suckers.
