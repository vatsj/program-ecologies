# Review of `specs/2026-10-05-k-four.md` by gpt-6.1-sol

## 1. Design flaws/confounds and fixes

- **The 4-rule’s soundness argument omits the crucial proof-cost contract.** From a witness of size ≤a, obtaining a proof of □ₐA within a+1 requires Nec to retain the witness’s cost and certify its bound. Ordinary necessitation alone does not establish this, especially for indexed boxes and proofs using the new rule. **Fix:** specify the proof encoding, cost recurrence, and indexed Nec rule; prove soundness inductively for the extended calculus, including boxed hypotheses and joint Löb. Treat “0 violations” as a regression test, not a soundness proof.

- **Part B cannot test its stated universal characterization.** Annotating one minimal GL derivation does not establish that *every* GL derivation uses the designated premises. Conversely, failure to translate that derivation does not establish K-underivability. **Fix:** distinguish an empirical proof-trace classifier from a theorem. For the theorem, supply a proof-normalization/completeness argument or a semantic invariant separating K from GL. Define “unprovable at level 0” independently of bounded search failure.

- **The supposed independent control remains post-selected.** The conjecture was motivated by K’s removed set; computing it from free-arm traces does not make its discovery independent. **Fix:** freeze the classifier now and evaluate on a held-out catalogue/cutoff. Report the confusion matrix over the *entire* eligible catalogue: evaluating chiefly the 37 removed classes cannot measure false positives adequately.

- **Changed equivalence classes could confound selection with mutation mass.** If classes are recomputed from K+4 play, comparing class counts or class-level chains can inadvertently change the prior. **Fix:** retain identical source programs and source-level mutation weights across arms; track splits/merges and aggregate their weights explicitly.

## 2. Predictions likely wrong

- **Prediction 3 is substantially overconfident.** Budget exhaustion, definition expansion, and missing rules besides 4 can prevent K proofs without any Gödel/Con premise. Equivalent GL proofs can also obscure the proposed syntactic trigger. **My prediction:** useful enrichment for disarmed classes, but counterexamples to the iff characterization; separate structural incompleteness from finite-budget failure.

- **Prediction 1’s reason for excluding P\*/P2 is insufficient.** Soundness does not forbid proving consistency of a weaker system or bounded consistency of the same system. Specify exactly which Con statement is required. **My prediction:** PB2 is the strongest re-arming candidate, but neither its ≤30 threshold nor P\*/P2’s permanent exclusion follows from the supplied argument.

- **Prediction 2’s chain bound is unsupported by faker counts.** Re-arming cooperators changes invasion targets and transition rates even if every old faker remains disarmed. **My prediction:** no justified ±0.02 forecast; new transition structure, rather than the old faker tally, will determine the sign.
