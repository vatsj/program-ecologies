# Review of `specs/2026-11-16-script-checked-offers.md` by gemini-4-carbon

1. **Design flaws or confounds**
- **Statement-relativity break between A3 and A4 (`S_off` compatibility).** Under the machine definition, `S_off(d)` means "if slot $d$'s accepted statements include `S_off` toward me... I name $d$ at ½." `Pc⁺`, however, demands *both* `S_off` and `S_nx` before counting $d$ eligible. Therefore `Pc⁺` *violates* `S_off(d)`: if $d$ is `Bc` (holds `S_off`, lacks `S_nx`), `Pc⁺` does not count $d$ eligible and does not name it at ½! If `CHKS` is sound (G1), `Pc⁺` cannot hold `S_off(d)`. It must instead hold a distinct conditional statement `S_off⁺(d)` ("if $d$ holds `S_off⁺` [or `S_off`] and `S_nx`..."). And if `Pc⁺` does not hold `S_off`, then in A4 (`Pc⁺+Oc⁺+Rc⁺`), `Oc⁺` and `Rc⁺` (whose requirement is `S_off` unless changed) will *not* recognize `Pc⁺`, or `Pc⁺` will pair with `Oc⁺` asymmetrically!
  - **Fix:** Parametrize the offer statement by its requirement, or define `S_off_req(R, d)` ("if $d$ holds every statement in $R$ toward me..."), and specify explicitly which requirement `Oc⁺` and `Rc⁺` use in A4, and whether `Pc⁺` accepts `S_off`+`S_nx` or `S_off⁺`+`S_nx`. Verify in G2 that `(Pc⁺, Pc⁺)`, `(Pc⁺, Oc⁺)`, and `(Pc⁺, Rc⁺)` all mutually pair at ½.
- **V3 action-equality vs. payoff-equality.** In queue 79, V3 passed on actions while 856 profiles differed by a $P_{\text{fail}} = 0.1$ timeout charge, altering selection rates (e.g., E4 falsified by charged residents: $\rho N = 5.9$ vs $1$). Here $\kappa_S$ in S1 is set to S0's *worst accepted* script cost, whereas rejected checks on non-carriers/forgers in S0 fail fast (a few ticks). Inside a simulated read of cap$' \ge 8\kappa_S$, S1 will overcharge rejected checks by orders of magnitude relative to S0, causing spurious read timeouts ($P_{\text{fail}}$ charges) or even changing fallback actions when a read times out in S1 that completed in S0.
  - **Fix:** Charge S1 primitives the exact static S0 step count per `(quote, script)` pair from a precomputed lookup table on the catalogue, rather than a uniform worst-case $\kappa_S$. Then V3 holds for both actions *and* charges.
- **Spelling distribution in A3/A4 is ambiguous.** A2 splits each family's mass over its 4+ spellings; A3 says "in A2's place," A4 says "`Pc⁺+Oc⁺+Rc⁺`" without stating whether A4 uses canonical spellings (like A1) or split spellings (like A2/A3).
  - **Fix:** Explicitly state that both A3 and A4 split mass across the same spelling set as A2, so A2 vs. A3 vs. A4 is unconfounded.

2. **Predictions I think are wrong**
- **RE 10 (simplest script carriers under `CHKS` primitive + script nodes are *at least as abundant* as the fair reader).** In queue 79, the 6-node fair reader had mass $\sim 1.3\times 10^{-11}$, and an 18-node R1-written carrier was $10^{-30}$ ($\sim 1.6$ orders of magnitude per syntax node). A step-by-step derivation script over $L_T$ evaluation rules proving behavior across all profiles—even for constant-fallback `Oc`—requires dozens of AST nodes of proof data. Counting script nodes under the length prior will push `Oc`/`Rc` well below 6 nodes (likely 25–100+ nodes, mass $\ll 10^{-30}$), falsifying the lower bound of RE 10 by many orders of magnitude. Prediction: with scripts counted as syntax/data nodes, script carriers are vastly rarer than the 6-node fair reader ($< 10^{-15}$ of a reader); they beat the reader only if the script sits outside the length-prior penalty (as an unpenalized gene).

3. **Missing controls or cheap additions**
- **`φ = 0` in A4 (`Pc⁺+Oc⁺+Rc⁺`).** Running A4 only at $\phi \in \{\frac{1}{2}, 1\}$ misses whether certified prudence has any intrinsic cost (e.g., fallback restrictions or compatibility) when all bullies are simulating ($\phi = 0$). Adding the 5 cells of A4 at $\beta = 3, \phi = 0, N = 200$ cleanly isolates the `Bc`-conversion loss (RE 8) from baseline carrier differences.
- **Step 0 assay for `(Pc⁺, Bc)` on `Bc`'s unequal island.** Queue 79's big surprise was `Fc` invading $e$ of `Bc`'s unequal island at $\rho N = 27.9$ by converting `Bc`. Explicitly assay `Pc⁺` into $e$ of `Bc`'s island (should drop back to $\rho N = 1$ since `Pc⁺` refuses `Bc`, leaving `Bc`'s unequal island intact!). That directly explains RE 8's mechanism: not only does `Bc` stop holding fair slots, its unequal islands stop opening in one step.

4. **Alternative explanations the brief does not rule out**
- **Fallback selection confound in A3/A4 vs. A2.** The brief notes "`Fc⁺` only for fallbacks for which `S_nx` is derivable." In queue 79, `Fc` used 16 firm readers from arm T; if some firm readers name $\frac{2}{3}$ off-path and are dropped in `Fc⁺`/`Pc⁺`, any shift between A2 and A3/A4 could be driven by restricting the fallback pool rather than `S_nx`. Fix: restrict A1/A2's `Fc` to the exact same two `S_nx`-derivable firm-reader fallbacks used in A3/A4.
